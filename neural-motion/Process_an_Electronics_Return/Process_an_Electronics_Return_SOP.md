# Process an Electronics Return SOP

One episode processes one returned device. It runs from one device lying on a bare inspect mat, one open bag
lying flat at the bag spot, a tape card, a cup of desiccant packs and three empty grade bins, to the device
taped, sealed in the bag with one desiccant pack, and lying in the grade bin that matches its grade, with
every tool back on its spot.

The order never changes: **look at the device, grade it, tape the two battery terminals, put the desiccant
pack in the bag, put the device in the bag, close the bag, then put the bag in its bin.** Nothing is graded
before every face has been looked at. Nothing is taped before it is graded. Nothing goes in the bag before the
tape is on. Nothing goes in a bin before the bag is closed.

**One gripper holds and the other gripper works.** The holding gripper is closed and empty and presses the
object down so it stays still. The working gripper picks up, carries, presses and places. The one two handed
action is the close: both grippers press the bag seal shut, each on its own end, and meet in the middle.
Nothing is ever passed from one gripper to the other, only one object is held at a time, and no grasp is
changed while an object is off the surface.

The device and the bag sit on the midline and are shared, so each gripper meets them from its own side.
Nothing is carried across the table to the far side, and no carry passes over another bin or over the open
bag.

The counts are fixed: **one device, one grade, two tape pieces, one for each terminal, one desiccant pack and
one bin.** There is no second grade and no third tape piece. The grade is never changed once the bag is
closed. Tape, a desiccant pack or a device that lands outside its place is left exactly where it falls,
tagged, and the episode continues. Nothing is ever picked up off the floor on camera.

## Setup

Complete both checklists before starting an episode.

### Cell configuration

* **Environment camera:** 90 mm.
* **cell_type:** bimanual

### Hardware checklist

1. Cameras are on and recording.
2. The environment camera frame holds the whole tabletop in one shot: the desiccant cup, the tape card spot,
   the bag spot, the inspect mat and all three grade bins.
3. Every face of the device stays in frame for the whole look, both battery terminals stay in frame while the
   tape goes on, and the letter on each grade bin is readable before the bag goes in.
4. Both arms are at home with their grippers open.
5. The tabletop is dry and clear of anything but the items in the Materials checklist.
6. Each arm reaches the tape card spot, the desiccant cup, the bag spot, the inspect mat and all three grade
   bins without extending to a joint limit or folding in on itself. Bring the bins forward until this is true.
7. Each arm reaches the device and the bag from its own side of the midline. Neither has to lean over the
   other to work them.
8. Neither arm needs the far side of the table for anything. The paths from the tape card and the cup forward
   to the mat, from the mat to the bag spot, and from the bag spot forward to the bins are clear. Carry the
   bag forward by hand during setup: it must reach every bin without passing over another bin. Widen the gaps
   between the bins until it does.

### Materials checklist

1. The inspect mat lies flat at the front center of the table, straddling the midline. It grips the table.
   Draw a hand across it during setup: if it slides, bunches or lifts, weight it or replace it with a rubber
   mat that stays put. Nothing pins the mat during the episode.
2. One device lies on the inspect mat, terminal side down, and nothing else is on the mat.
3. The device is one dry unit, light enough for one gripper to carry level, and square enough for the gripper
   to close on it and hold it level. It is not switched on and it has no cable on it.
4. The device carries one mock battery pack, fixed flat to one face, with two flat metal terminals side by
   side on it. The pack is a training part. It holds no charge and no cells. A device with a real cell in it
   is not usable.
5. The two terminals are bare, dry and clean, far enough apart that one tape piece covers one terminal without
   touching the other, and each one is smaller than one tape piece.
6. The device shows the marks its grade is read from. The same device is not used twice in a row without a
   fresh reset.
7. One tape card lies flat on the tape card spot at the back right. It carries at least two pre cut tape
   pieces, each one lifted at a corner so a gripper can peel it. Nothing on this station cuts, so tape on a
   roll is not usable.
8. Each tape piece is wide enough to cover one terminal with a clear edge all the way round, and it sticks
   down in one press. A piece that curls back on itself is replaced.
9. One cup of desiccant packs stands at the back left. It holds more than one pack. Each pack is dry, sealed,
   flat and small enough to lie beside the device in the bag.
10. One bag lies flat and open at the bag spot, left of the midline, its mouth toward the front of the table.
    It is a slide seal bag. It stays open on its own, it is wide enough for the device to go in without being
    pushed, and it is clear enough that the device and the pack are visible through it.
11. Three grade bins stand in a row across the front of the table, marked A, B and C from left to right, with
    a clear gap between them. All three are empty, shallow enough that the bag is visible inside, and wide
    enough that the bag goes in without being pushed.

### Workspace layout

Three rows: the tape card and the desiccant cup at the back, the bag and the inspect mat in the middle, the
three grade bins at the front. Everything travels forward toward the bins.

Back row, left to right:

1. Desiccant cup, back left.
2. Tape card spot, back right, the card lying flat with its pieces facing up.

Middle row, left to right:

3. Bag spot, left of the midline, the bag lying flat and open with its mouth toward the front. Shared: one
   gripper holds it, the other works it, and each meets it from its own side.
4. Inspect mat, center, straddling the midline. The device lies there from the start of the episode until
   Step 3.

Front row, left to right:

5. Grade bins, across the front, marked A, B and C from left to right. All empty at the start, one closed bag
   in one of them at the end.

A clear gap of at least a gripper's width sits between the bag spot and the inspect mat, and between each pair
of bins, so an object lifts straight up and travels forward without touching anything. No carry passes over
another bin, over the open bag or over the tape card.

## Vocabulary

* **Hold:** a closed empty gripper pressed onto an object to keep it still. A hold never lifts, carries or
  moves what it holds.
* **Work:** the other gripper picking up, carrying, pressing or placing. One gripper holds and the other
  works, and the two never trade jobs part way through a step.
* **Home spot:** the spot an object is laid on and lies flat on before the next step starts: the tape card
  spot, the desiccant cup, the bag spot, the inspect mat, a grade bin. Nothing is left on bare tabletop or in
  a gripper between steps.
* **Release point:** the gripper opens over the inspect mat, over the bag spot, or inside the mouth of a grade
  bin. Never over another bin, never over the tape card and never over open tabletop.
* **Stable placement:** the object lies still after release, flat on its surface, and does not rock, tip,
  slide or lean on anything.
* **Device:** the single returned unit. It is the thing that is graded, taped and bagged.
* **Face:** one side of the device. Every face is brought into the camera frame in Step 1.
* **Face look:** one face brought into the camera frame and held still for 1 second. The device is kept low
  over the mat and is set back down between faces.
* **Mock battery pack:** the training battery pack fixed flat to one face of the device. It holds no charge.
  It is never taken off the device.
* **Terminal:** one of the two flat metal pads on the mock battery pack. There are two, and each one gets its
  own tape piece.
* **Tape piece:** one pre cut piece of tape from the tape card. It is taken by its lifted corner, never by its
  sticky face.
* **Terminal covered:** one tape piece lying flat over one terminal, with no metal showing at any edge and no
  air bubble under it.
* **Desiccant pack:** the small sealed pack that goes in the bag to keep the device dry. One pack per bag.
* **Bag:** the clear slide seal bag the device is sealed in. It stays flat on the bag spot until it is closed.
* **Closed bag:** the seal pressed shut along its whole length, with no gap at either end and no corner of the
  device or the pack caught in the seal.
* **Grade:** the letter A, B or C that says what state the device is in. One grade per episode, chosen once.
* **Grade A:** no crack, no dent, no missing part, no water mark, and no scratch deep enough to feel. The
  device looks new.
* **Grade B:** light marks or scratches only. Nothing is broken, nothing is bent and no part is missing.
* **Grade C:** any crack, any dent, any bent edge, any missing or loose part, or any water mark. One Grade C
  sign is enough.
* **Lower grade wins:** when a device sits between two grades, take the lower one. A is the highest and C is
  the lowest.
* **Spill:** a tape piece, a desiccant pack or a device that ends up outside its place. It is left where it
  falls and cleared in the reset, never on camera.
* **Quarter turn:** the most any object is tilted in a gripper. Turning a face up and laying it back down both
  stay inside it. Nothing is ever turned over in the air.

## Steps

### Step 1: Look at the device and grade it

**Goal:** every face of the device has been seen in the camera frame, one grade has been chosen, and the
device lies flat on the mat again.

The device stays on the inspect mat for this whole step. It is never carried off the mat and never held up in
the air.

#### 1.1 Look at every face

* With one gripper, hold the device flat on the mat so it stays still.
* With the other gripper, close on the device, lift it just clear of the mat, and turn it **no more than a
  quarter turn** to show one face to the camera.
* Hold that face still for **1 second**, then lay the device back down flat on the mat.
* Do this for **every face**, one at a time, until every face has been shown. Set the device down between
  faces.

The device is kept low over the mat the whole time. A device turned over in the air is out of frame for part
of the turn, and a face that is never framed cannot be graded.

**Check:** every face has been in the camera frame and held still for 1 second, and the device lies flat on
the mat. If a face was missed or was blurred, show that face once more. Make no more than two extra looks.

#### 1.2 Choose the grade

* Choose **A, B or C** from what was seen, using the rules in the Vocabulary.
* Choose once. **The grade is not changed later in the episode.**
* If the device sits between two grades, **take the lower one**.

**If any Grade C sign is visible, the grade is C**, even if the rest of the device looks new.

**Check:** one grade has been chosen and it matches what is visible on the device. If nothing on the device
can be read clearly, grade it C and let review flag the episode.

### Step 2: Tape the battery terminals

**Goal:** both terminals are covered, one tape piece on each, and the tape card is back flat on its spot.

The device is held down for the whole of this step. Tape is pressed onto a held device, never onto a loose
one.

#### 2.1 Turn the terminal side up

* With one gripper, close on the device, lift it just clear of the mat, and turn it no more than a quarter
  turn so the mock battery pack faces up.
* Lay the device back down flat on the mat and release.

**Check:** the device lies flat on the mat with both terminals facing up and both in the camera frame. If a
terminal is hidden or the device rocks, lift it clear and lay it down again. Make no more than one correction.

#### 2.2 Cover the first terminal

* With one gripper closed and empty, come down on the device beside the battery pack and press it against the
  mat. Take this hold **before the tape comes over**, and keep it **until both pieces are on**.
* With the other gripper, take one tape piece from the tape card **by its lifted corner**, sticky side down.
* Carry it over the first terminal, lower it until it lies flat over the metal, and press it down once.
* Release the tape and lift clear.

**Never take a tape piece by its sticky face.** A piece taken flat on its sticky side folds onto itself and
cannot be laid down.

**Check:** the tape lies flat over the terminal, no metal shows at any edge, and there is no bubble under it.
If metal still shows, press the same piece down again over the open edge. Make no more than one correction.
Never lay a second piece on top of the first.

#### 2.3 Cover the second terminal

* Keep the hold on the device.
* Take the **second** tape piece from the tape card the same way and lay it over the second terminal.
* Release the tape, lift clear, and **only now** release the device.

**Two pieces in total, one for each terminal.** Nothing else changes between them.

**Check:** both terminals are covered, each by its own piece, no metal shows, and the tape card lies flat on
its spot. A piece that will not stick is not replaced with a third piece: leave it and let review flag the
episode.

### Step 3: Bag the device with a desiccant pack

**Goal:** one desiccant pack and the taped device lie inside the bag, the bag is closed, and the bag lies flat
on the bag spot.

The bag stays flat on the bag spot for this whole step. It is never lifted, held up or turned over while it is
open. The pack goes in first, so the device is not lifted twice.

#### 3.1 Put in the desiccant pack

* With one gripper closed and empty, come down on the bag beside its mouth and press it against the table so
  it stays open and still.
* With the other gripper, take **one** desiccant pack from the cup and lift it straight up clear of the rim.
* Carry it to the bag, bring it inside the mouth, lower it until it lies flat on the bottom of the bag, and
  release.
* Lift clear of the mouth.

**One pack, and one only.** The pack goes in before the device so it is not trapped under it.

**Check:** exactly one pack lies flat inside the bag and the bag still lies open on its spot. If a second pack
came out of the cup, put it back in the cup before going on.

#### 3.2 Put in the device

* Keep the hold on the bag.
* With the other gripper, close on the device on the mat, lift it straight up clear of the mat, and carry it
  level to the bag mouth.
* Bring the device inside the mouth, lower it until it lies flat beside the pack, and release.
* Lift clear of the mouth.

The device slides in on its own. A device that has to be pushed means the bag is too small: end the episode
and log a defective bag.

**Check:** the device lies flat inside the bag with the pack beside it, the tape is still on both terminals,
and nothing hangs out of the mouth. If part of the device is still outside the mouth, lift it clear and put it
in again. Make no more than one correction.

#### 3.3 Close the bag

* Bring both grippers down on the bag seal, one on each end.
* Press the seal shut **along its whole length**, each gripper working from its own end toward the middle,
  until the two meet.
* Release and lift clear.

**Both ends move together.** A seal pressed from one end only leaves the far corner open. The seal is long,
and one gripper cannot cover it in one press.

**Check:** the seal is shut along its whole length, no gap shows at either end, nothing is caught in the seal,
and the bag lies flat on its spot. If a corner is still open, press that corner once more. Make no more than
one correction. If the seal will not hold, end the episode and log a defective bag.

### Step 4: Put the bag in its grade bin

**Goal:** the closed bag lies in the bin marked with the grade chosen in Step 1, and nothing is in the other
two bins.

* With one gripper, close on the bag near its sealed edge, square to it so it hangs level.
* Lift it straight up clear of the table before any sideways move.
* Carry it level and forward to the bin marked with the **chosen grade**, going around the other bins, **never
  over them**.
* Bring it inside the mouth of the bin, lower it until it rests on the bottom, and release.
* Lift clear of the bin.

**The bag is lowered in, never dropped.** A bag let go above the bin lands on its edge and can slide out.

**Check:** the bag lies flat inside the bin whose letter matches the chosen grade, the other two bins are
empty, and no part of the bag hangs over the rim. If the bag hangs over a rim, lift it clear and place it
again. Make no more than one correction. A bag placed in the wrong bin is not moved: leave it and let review
flag the episode.

### Step 5: End the episode

* Confirm the device: both terminals covered, one tape piece on each, no metal showing.
* Confirm the bag: closed along its whole length, holding the device and one desiccant pack.
* Confirm the bins: the bag in the bin that matches the grade, the other two bins empty.
* Confirm the tools: the tape card flat on its spot, the desiccant cup upright on its spot, the inspect mat
  bare and flat.
* Correct a failed check only with the correction written in its step. If no correction is written, or the
  correction would need an earlier step run again, end the episode and reset instead.
* Return both arms home with their grippers open, then stop recording.

## After the episode: reset the workspace

This reset is not recorded.

1. Take the bag out of its bin, open the seal, take out the device and the desiccant pack, and empty all three
   bins.
2. Peel both tape pieces off the terminals, bin the used tape, and wipe the terminals dry and clean.
3. Lay the device flat on the inspect mat, terminal side down, and leave nothing else on the mat.
4. Lay a fresh bag flat and open at the bag spot with its mouth toward the front. A bag whose seal has been
   used is not used again.
5. Put the used desiccant pack aside and top up the cup so it holds more than one dry pack.
6. Lay a fresh tape card flat on its spot with at least two pre cut pieces on it, each one lifted at a corner.
7. Pick up any tape, pack or device that fell during the episode, and wipe the tabletop and the mat dry.
8. Confirm the mat lies flat and still grips the table, the mock battery pack is still fixed flat to the
   device, no bin is cracked or leaning, and no bag in the stack is torn.
9. Run both Setup checklists again.

## SOP violations

These are actions that break the SOP and are reviewed side by side in the review tool.

### How to record a violation in review

For each violation, record the start timestamp, violation name, and SOP rule broken. The visible cue is what
the reviewer sees. The coaching note is for retraining and is not an annotation label.

### Episode handling

Tag every violation with its timestamp and name. Keep the episode with the violation tag. Do not delete it
just because a rule was broken.

### Violations

#### Violation: Wrong order

* **Visible cue:** tape is taken before every face has been looked at, the device goes into the bag before
  both terminals are covered, the desiccant pack goes in after the device, the bag is carried to a bin before
  its seal is shut, or a step is skipped.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 4, look and grade, tape, bag, bin, in that order, with the pack in before
  the device and the seal shut before the carry.
* **Coaching note:** look it, grade it, tape it, bag it, bin it. The pack goes in first and the seal goes on
  last.

#### Violation: Faces not all looked at

* **Visible cue:** a grade is chosen while one or more faces have never been in the camera frame, a face is
  shown for less than 1 second, or a face is shown while the device is moving.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 1.1, every face is brought into the camera frame and held still for 1 second.
* **Coaching note:** every face, one at a time, still for a full second. A face that was never framed cannot
  be graded.

#### Violation: Device turned over in the air or carried off the mat

* **Visible cue:** the device is rolled past a quarter turn in a gripper, is turned over in the air, is held
  high above the mat, or leaves the inspect mat before Step 3.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 and 2, the device is turned no more than a quarter turn, kept low over the mat,
  and set back down between faces.
* **Coaching note:** quarter turn, low over the mat, down between faces. The mat is where the device lives
  until it is bagged.

#### Violation: Wrong grade for what is visible

* **Visible cue:** a crack, dent, bent edge, missing part or water mark is visible on camera and the device is
  graded A or B, or a device with light marks only is graded C.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 1.2, the grade matches what is visible, and where the device sits between two
  grades the lower one is taken.
* **Coaching note:** one Grade C sign is enough for C. When in doubt, take the lower grade.

#### Violation: Grade changed after it is chosen

* **Visible cue:** the bag is carried toward one bin and then taken to another, or the bag is lifted back out
  of a bin and put in a different one.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1.2 and 4, one grade is chosen once and the bag goes to that bin only.
* **Coaching note:** the grade is settled before the tape goes on. A bag in the wrong bin is left there and
  flagged, not moved.

#### Violation: Device not held while the tape goes on

* **Visible cue:** the holding gripper is off the device at any point between the first tape piece being taken
  and the second piece being pressed down, or the device shifts, slides or lifts as the tape is pressed.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2.2 and 2.3, one gripper holds the device against the mat until both pieces are
  on.
* **Coaching note:** the holding gripper holds through both pieces, not just the first. Tape goes onto a
  device that cannot move.

#### Violation: Tape count wrong, or tape taken wrong

* **Visible cue:** fewer or more than two tape pieces are used, one piece is laid over both terminals, a
  second piece is laid on top of the first, or a piece is taken flat on its sticky face instead of by its
  lifted corner.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 2, two pieces in total, one for each terminal, each taken by its lifted corner.
* **Coaching note:** two pieces, one per terminal, taken by the corner. A third piece makes the episode
  unlearnable.

#### Violation: Terminal still showing after the tape

* **Visible cue:** metal shows at an edge of a tape piece, a piece sits half on and half off a terminal, a
  piece is folded on itself, or a piece lifts back off after it is pressed.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 2, each tape piece lies flat over its terminal with no metal showing at any edge.
* **Coaching note:** lower it flat, then press once. Check the edges before the gripper leaves.

#### Violation: Desiccant pack missing, doubled, or put in late

* **Visible cue:** the bag is closed with no pack inside, with two or more packs inside, or the pack goes into
  the bag after the device.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3.1, one desiccant pack goes into the bag, and it goes in before the device.
* **Coaching note:** one pack, and it goes in first. If two come out of the cup, one goes back before the
  device moves.

#### Violation: Bag lifted, held open, or pushed

* **Visible cue:** the bag is lifted off its spot, held up in the air, or turned over while it is still open;
  the device or the pack is pushed, shoved or forced through the mouth; or the holding gripper is off the bag
  while something is going into it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3, the bag stays flat on its spot while it is open, one gripper holds it, and
  everything goes in without being pushed.
* **Coaching note:** the bag is worked flat on the table. If it has to be pushed, the bag is wrong, not the
  motion.

#### Violation: Bag not closed, or closed with a gap

* **Visible cue:** the seal is pressed by one gripper only, a corner of the seal is left open, the bag goes to
  a bin with the seal still open, or the device or the pack is caught in the seal.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3.3, both grippers press the seal from their own end toward the middle until it is
  shut along its whole length.
* **Coaching note:** both ends, meeting in the middle. Look at both corners before the bag is lifted.

#### Violation: Bag put in the wrong bin

* **Visible cue:** the bag ends in a bin whose letter is not the grade chosen in Step 1, or in more than one
  bin during the carry.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 4, the bag goes in the bin marked with the chosen grade.
* **Coaching note:** read the bin letter before the gripper opens.

#### Violation: Bag dropped into the bin

* **Visible cue:** the gripper opens while the bag is still above the bin rim, the bag falls a visible
  distance, or the bag lands on edge and slides.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 4, the bag is lowered until it rests on the bottom of the bin, then released.
* **Coaching note:** lower until it touches, then open. A drop is not a placement.

#### Violation: A carry passes over another bin or over the open bag

* **Visible cue:** the bag passes over a bin that is not its own, a tape piece or a desiccant pack passes over
  the open bag on its way somewhere else, or an object is carried across to the far side of the table.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2 to 4, every carry goes around the other bins and around the open bag, never
  over them.
* **Coaching note:** route around, not across. Anything dropped over an open bag or an open bin lands in the
  wrong place.

#### Violation: Spill cleaned up

* **Visible cue:** after a tape piece, a desiccant pack or the device lands outside its place, a gripper is
  used to sweep, push, brush or pick it back up.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2 to 4, a spill is left where it falls and is cleared in the reset.
* **Coaching note:** cleanup motions are not part of the task. The spill is data, the cleanup is noise.

#### Violation: Bad pick, carry, or release

* **Visible cue:** an object moves from one gripper to the other without being set down first, both grippers
  hold an object at once outside the two handed close in Step 3.3, a gripper shifts or seats its hold again
  while the object is off the table, the device or the bag slides along the table with no daylight under it,
  the device hangs tilted in the gripper because the grasp was taken off square, or an object is rolled past a
  quarter turn or turned over in the air.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 4, one object at a time, lifted straight up clear of the surface before any
  sideways motion, gripped square, tilted no more than a quarter turn, and never passed between grippers.
* **Coaching note:** set it down to change the grip, and lift before the carry. A device that hangs tilted was
  taken off square.

#### Violation: Placement pushed, or correction limit exceeded

* **Visible cue:** the device, the bag, a bin, the tape card or the mat is shoved, nudged or slid into
  position instead of being lifted clear and placed again, or a failed check is retried more often than its
  step allows.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 4, a bad placement is corrected by lifting the object clear and placing it
  again, and each check allows only the correction it states.
* **Coaching note:** nothing on this station is pushed into line. If grip or approach is forcing repeated
  correction, let review flag it and move on.

#### Violation: Dropped, collided, or knocked over

* **Visible cue:** under command, a gripper or a carried object strikes a bin, the cup, the tape card, the bag
  or the mat and knocks, tips, slides or displaces it; the two arms strike each other over the device or the
  bag; or an object falls short of its spot, with no gripper failure, drift, or controller fault visible.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 4, keep each object on a clear travel path and release only after a settled
  placement.
* **Coaching note:** watch the whole path, not just the target. When one gripper holds, the holding one stays
  put and only the working one moves.

#### Violation: Manipulation outside the camera frame

* **Visible cue:** a face look, a tape press, a bag close, or a bin placement happens partly or wholly outside
  the environment camera frame.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 4, every manipulation happens inside the camera frame.
* **Coaching note:** the work has drifted outside the framed area. Set the spots back to their setup
  positions.

#### Violation: Wrong episode ending

* **Visible cue:** the episode ends with a terminal uncovered, the bag open, the bag still on the bag spot,
  the device still on the mat, a tool out of its spot, an arm short of home, or a gripper not fully open.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 5, confirm the device, the bag, the bins and the tools, then return both arms home
  with grippers open and stop recording.
* **Coaching note:** confirm first. Homing is the last thing the arms do before the recording ends.

## Failures that are not violations

These failures are not caused by how the task was run. Log them as system issues, discard the episode, and
never use them for coaching.

* Recording stopped or paused during the episode (recording system).
* Camera dropped frames or lost its feed (capture system).
* Hardware fault on an arm: gripper failure, drift, controller caused collision, or motor error.
* A spot sits outside an arm's comfortable reach, so the arm stalls at a joint limit or has to fold in on
  itself to work it. Move the spot and run the Setup checklists again.
* Defective mat: one that slides, bunches or lifts under a correct carry, or that will not lie flat. Weight or
  replace it before the next episode.
* Defective tape card: one whose pieces tear when peeled, whose pieces have no lifted corner, or that has
  fewer than two pieces left.
* Defective tape: a piece that will not stick to a clean dry terminal, or that curls back on itself as it is
  lifted.
* Defective bag: one that will not stay open on its own, whose seal will not shut or will not hold, that is
  torn, or that is too small for the device to go in without being pushed.
* Damp, split or already used desiccant pack at episode start.
* Defective bin: one too deep to see the bag in, too narrow for the bag to go in without being pushed, or that
  will not stand still.
* Loose or missing mock battery pack, or terminals that are wet, dirty or bent at episode start.
* Device too heavy or too large for one gripper to carry level, or a device that arrives switched on or with a
  cable still on it. Reset error, not a violation.

## Annotation subtasks (from SOP)

1. Hold the device steady on the mat
2. Turn the device to show one face
3. Lay the device back on the mat
4. Turn the device terminal side up
5. Take one tape piece from the tape card
6. Press one tape piece over one terminal
7. Hold the bag open on its spot
8. Take one desiccant pack from the cup
9. Put the desiccant pack in the bag
10. Lift the device from the inspect mat
11. Put the device in the bag
12. Press the bag seal shut with both grippers
13. Lift the closed bag from the bag spot
14. Place the bag in its grade bin
15. Return both arms home and end the episode
