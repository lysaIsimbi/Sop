# Return Tools to the Shadow Board SOP (1x Episode: five tools, one missing slot, in situ)

One episode puts **five tools** back on the wall board where they live. The tools come from a tray on the
bench. Each tool is wiped with the cloth, then hung on the board inside the outline that has its shape. When
all five tools hang on the board, one outline is still empty. That is the missing slot. You hang the red tag
on it. The episode ends when the tray is empty, five tools hang inside their outlines, the tag hangs on the
empty outline, and the cloth is back where it started. Never split the tools into two episodes.

The base is **passive**. It has no motor. You push it by hand to the bench in front of the wall board and lock
it there. Nothing is carried away to another table. Everything the episode touches is already there when
recording starts: the wall board with its outlines and hooks, the tray of tools on the bench, the cloth on its
spot, and the red tag on its spot.

The board is used **as found**. It has six outlines, one for each tool, and one hook inside each outline. At
the start, all six outlines are empty. The tray holds five tools. One tool is not there. Which tool is missing
changes from episode to episode. You do not know which one until the board tells you. The episode ends with
five outlines filled, one outline flagged, and the tray empty.

**This is an in situ task. Four rules come from that.** First, the board is on the wall, above the bench, so
every tool goes up to the board and never comes back down. Once a tool hangs on the board, nobody touches it
again. Second, **you wipe before you hang**. Every tool is wiped with the cloth before it goes up. No tool
goes on the board dirty. Third, **the shape decides the slot**. You match the tool to the outline that has its
shape. You do not go by the order of the hooks. You do not go by where the last tool went. Fourth, **one tool
at a time**. One tool leaves the tray, is wiped, and is hung before the next tool leaves the tray.

**The board and the tray never move.** Nobody leans on, pushes, drags, or lifts the board, a hook, or the
tray. A tool that falls on the bench or on the floor is left where it falls. You do not pick it up on camera.
You report it.

The order never changes: **take a tool from the tray, wipe it, match it, hang it. Do that five times. Then
find the empty outline and hang the tag on it. Then put the cloth back.**

## Setup

Do the base positioning and both checklists before you start an episode.

### Cell configuration

* **cell_type:** bimanual

### Base positioning

The base is **passive**. It has no motor. You push it into place by hand. You never steer it, nudge it, or
move it after recording starts. You park it once, before recording, and it stays there until the episode is
over.

1. Push the base by hand up to the bench. Stop it **square to the front edge of the bench**, so the front edge
   runs straight across the frame and both ends look the same distance away.
2. Stop it **in the middle of the board**, so the left end and the right end of the board are the same
   distance from the middle of the base.
3. Stop it **close enough** that a gripper reaches every hook on the board without stretching the arm. Stop it
   **far enough** that no arm, wrist, or part of the base touches the bench front, the tray, or the board
   while both arms work.
4. Check the **height**. With the base parked, a gripper lifts a tool out of the tray and brings it up to the
   top row of hooks. The wrist and the forearm must not hit the board or the bench.
5. Check the **tray**. A gripper reaches every tool in the tray, from the left end to the right end, without
   stretching and without knocking the tray.
6. Check the **cloth and the tag**. A gripper reaches the cloth on its spot and the tag on its spot without
   stretching and without knocking anything over.
7. Lock the base. Push it once, hard, by hand. It must not roll, slide, or turn.
8. If any line from 1 to 6 fails, push the base to a new spot by hand and start again at line 1. Do not work
   at a board the arms cannot reach easily.

**The base stays locked and still for the whole episode.** Nothing moves it. No arm leans on the bench hard
enough to push it. Nobody touches it by hand. You never move it during the task. A base that moves after
recording starts ends the episode.

### Hardware checklist

1. Cameras are on and recording.
2. The camera points at the middle of the board. The frame shows the whole board with all six outlines and all
   six hooks, the tray with all its tools, the cloth on its spot, and the tag on its spot.
3. The camera sees the board from the front, so you can see the shape of each outline and if a tool hangs
   inside it.
4. The camera sees each hook, so you can see if a tool is on its hook or only leaning on the board.
5. The camera sees the inside of the tray from above the front edge, so you can see how many tools are left in
   it.
6. The camera sees the cloth and the tag, so you can see the wipe and see where the tag hangs at the end.
7. Both arms are at home with the grippers open.
8. The arms reach every tool in the tray, every hook on the board, the cloth, and the tag. They reach all of
   them without stretching to a limit.
9. A gripper lifts a tool out of the tray and carries it up to the board. It never carries a tool over the
   cloth or over the tag. It never carries a tool behind another tool that already hangs on the board.
10. The two arms do not hit each other. An arm at the board stays out of the other arm's path to the tray.
11. If the arms cannot reach a place, park the base again with the Base positioning steps until lines 8 to 10
    are all true.

### Materials checklist

1. The board is a flat board fixed to the wall above the back of the bench. Nobody moves it, leans on it, or
   hangs anything else on it.
2. The board has six outlines painted on it. Each outline is the shape of one tool, painted in a dark colour
   on a light board, so you can see the shape from the front.
3. The six outlines are: a hammer, a screwdriver, a pair of pliers, a spanner, a tape measure, and a spirit
   level. No two outlines have the same shape.
4. Each outline has one hook inside it. The hook is fixed to the board. It does not turn, bend, or come out.
5. The outlines do not touch each other. There is a clear gap between every two outlines, so a tool on one
   hook does not cover the outline next to it.
6. At the start, all six hooks are empty. Nothing hangs on the board.
7. The tray is an open tray with low sides. It stands on the bench in front of the board, at the left end.
   Nobody moves it or lifts it.
8. The tray holds five tools. They lie side by side, in one row, from the left end of the tray to the right
   end. They do not lie on top of each other. They do not touch each other.
9. Each tool in the tray is one of the six tools on the board. No tool in the tray is a tool the board has no
   outline for.
10. One tool from the six is not in the tray. Which one is missing is set before the episode, and it changes
    from episode to episode. The tools in the tray are laid in the tray in any order.
11. Each tool has one hole or one loop that it hangs by. The hole or loop fits the hook on the board. When the
    tool hangs on its hook, it hangs still and stays inside its outline.
12. Each tool has dust on it at the start. The dust is plain to see on camera, and it comes off with one wipe
    of the cloth.
13. The cloth is a folded, dry cloth. It lies flat on the bench on the cloth spot, right of the tray, out of
    the way of the tray.
14. The tag is a red tag on a loop of string. It says **MISSING** in big letters. It lies flat on the bench on
    the tag spot, right of the cloth, with its loop toward the front.
15. The wipe spot is the clear strip of bench between the tray and the cloth. Every tool is wiped above it.
    Nothing is put down in it.
16. The bench is dry and clear of anything but the tray, the cloth, and the tag.

## Workspace layout

Nothing is marked or taped out. You judge every place below by eye, from the board and the bench themselves.

* **Board:** the wall board above the back of the bench. Six outlines, six hooks. Tools go up to it and never
  come back down.
* **Outline:** one painted shape on the board with one hook inside it. Each tool has one outline and no other.
* **Tray:** the open tray at the left end of the bench. Five tools start in it. It is empty at the end. Never
  moved.
* **Wipe spot:** the clear strip of bench between the tray and the cloth. Every tool is wiped above it.
  Nothing is ever put down in it.
* **Cloth spot:** where the cloth lies flat, right of the wipe spot. The cloth starts and ends here.
* **Tag spot:** where the red tag lies flat, right of the cloth. The tag starts here and ends on the board.
* **Missing slot:** the one outline that is still empty when all five tools hang on the board. The tag goes on
  its hook.

## Vocabulary

* **Outline:** the painted shape of one tool on the board. It is the same size as the tool. It shows which way
  the tool hangs.
* **Hook:** the peg inside each outline that the tool hangs from.
* **Match:** the tool and the outline have the same shape. A hammer goes on the hammer outline. A spanner goes
  on the spanner outline. Nothing else counts as a match.
* **Next tool:** the tool in the tray that is farthest to the left. Tools leave the tray from left to right.
* **Wiped:** the cloth has gone over both faces of the tool, and the dust is gone. The tool looks clean from
  the front.
* **Hanging right:** the tool hangs from its hook by its hole or loop, it hangs still, and its whole shape
  sits inside the outline the same way round as the outline. No part of it sticks out over the paint.
* **Hanging wrong:** the tool is on the wrong outline, hangs upside down, hangs turned, sticks out over the
  paint, leans on the board instead of hanging from the hook, or swings.
* **Missing slot:** the one outline with no tool on its hook when the tray is empty.
* **Flagged:** the red tag hangs from the hook inside the missing slot, with the word MISSING facing the
  front.
* **Dropped:** a tool or the tag leaves the gripper and lands on the bench or the floor. You do not pick it
  up. You report it.
* **Back and clear:** the arm is pulled back, so no part of it is over the tray, the wipe spot, or in front of
  the board.

## Steps

### Step 1: Take the next tool from the tray

**Goal:** one tool is out of the tray and held still above the wipe spot.

#### 1.1 Find the next tool

* Look in the tray. The next tool is the one farthest to the left.
* Do not pick the biggest tool, or the nearest one, or the one you like. Always the one on the left.

#### 1.2 Lift it out

* Close on the tool by its handle, or by its body if it has no handle. Do not close on the part it hangs by.
* Lift it straight up, just clear of the tray sides. Do not drag it along the tray floor. Do not knock the
  tools next to it.
* Bring it right, over the wipe spot, and hold it still.

**Check:** one tool is held still above the wipe spot. The tray has not moved. The other tools in the tray
have not moved. If the tool slipped or hangs loose in the gripper, put it back down in the tray where it was,
take it again, and lift again.

**Expected state:** one tool is above the wipe spot, held still. The tray holds one tool fewer. The cloth and
the tag are on their spots.

### Step 2: Wipe the tool

**Goal:** the tool is clean on both faces, and the cloth is back on its spot.

#### 2.1 Pick up the cloth

* Close on the cloth at its near edge. Lift it just off the bench.
* Bring it to the tool above the wipe spot.

#### 2.2 Wipe both faces

* Hold the tool still. Wipe the cloth along the front face of the tool, from one end to the other.
* Turn the tool over. Wipe the cloth along the back face, from one end to the other.
* Wipe with the cloth flat. Do not bunch it into a ball. Wipe gently. Do not push the tool out of the gripper.

**Check:** the tool is **wiped**. No dust on either face. If a dusty spot is left, wipe that spot again.

#### 2.3 Put the cloth back

* Lay the cloth back flat on the cloth spot where it started.
* Open the gripper on the cloth and pull it back.

**Check:** the cloth lies flat on its spot. The tool is still held above the wipe spot. Nothing has been put
down in the wipe spot.

**Expected state:** the tool is clean and held still above the wipe spot. The cloth is on its spot. The tray
and the tag have not moved.

### Step 3: Match the tool to its outline

**Goal:** you know which outline on the board has the shape of the tool.

#### 3.1 Look at the tool

* Look at the shape of the tool in the gripper. Is it a hammer, a screwdriver, a pair of pliers, a spanner, a
  tape measure, or a spirit level?

#### 3.2 Look at the board

* Look at the six outlines on the board. Find the one outline with the same shape as the tool.
* Go by the shape only. Do not go by the order of the hooks. Do not go by the empty hook nearest to you. Do
  not go by where the last tool went.
* Look at which way the outline points. That is the way the tool hangs.

**Check:** one outline, and only one, has the shape of the tool. Its hook is empty. If you are not sure, hold
the tool up in front of the outline, close to the board but not touching it, and compare. If the shape does
not fit, try the next outline. Do not hang a tool on an outline that is only close.

**Expected state:** the tool is held still. You have picked one outline for it. Nothing has touched the board
yet.

### Step 4: Hang the tool

**Goal:** the tool hangs from the hook inside its outline, the same way round as the outline.

#### 4.1 Bring it up to the board

* Carry the tool up to the board and in front of its outline. Keep it clear of the tools that already hang on
  the board.
* Turn the tool so its hole or loop is at the top, the same way round as the outline shows.

#### 4.2 Put it on the hook

* Bring the hole or loop over the hook. Lower the tool straight down until the hook holds it.
* Let the tool settle. Wait until it hangs still.

#### 4.3 Let go

* Open the gripper and pull it straight back from the board. Do not brush the tool as you leave.
* Do not touch this tool again for the rest of the episode.

**Check:** the tool is **hanging right**. It hangs from the hook, hangs still, and its whole shape sits inside
the outline with no part over the paint. If it swings, wait for it to stop. If it is on the wrong outline,
turned, upside down, or sticking out, close on it again, lift it straight off the hook, and hang it again. Fix
it now. Do not leave it for later.

**Expected state:** one more tool hangs on the board inside its outline. The gripper is back and clear. The
tray, the cloth, and the tag have not moved.

### Step 5: Do the rest of the tools

**Goal:** all five tools hang on the board, each inside its outline, and the tray is empty.

* Go back to Step 1. Take the next tool, the one now farthest to the left in the tray.
* Do Steps 1 to 4 for that tool. Then do them again for the next tool, until the tray is empty.
* One tool at a time. Do not lift the next tool while one is still held, still being wiped, or not yet hanging
  right.
* Wipe every tool. Do not skip the wipe on a tool that looks clean.

**Check:** the tray is empty. Five tools hang on the board. Each one hangs right inside its own outline. Count
the tools on the board. If you count four, a tool is on the bench or the floor. Stop and report it. If two
tools are on one hook, or a tool is on the wrong outline, fix it with the retry in Step 4.

**Expected state:** five tools hang right on the board. One outline is empty. The tray is empty. The cloth is
on its spot. The tag is on its spot.

### Step 6: Flag the missing slot

**Goal:** the red tag hangs on the hook inside the one empty outline, with MISSING facing the front.

#### 6.1 Find the empty outline

* Look along the board. Find the one outline with nothing on its hook.
* There is exactly one. If you see two empty outlines, a tool is not hung. Go back to Step 5. If you see none,
  a tool is on the wrong outline. Go back to Step 4.

#### 6.2 Pick up the tag

* Close on the tag by its body, not by its string. Lift it just off the bench.
* Carry it up to the board and in front of the empty outline. Keep it clear of the tools on the board.

#### 6.3 Hang the tag

* Bring the loop of string over the hook. Lower the tag until the hook holds the loop.
* Turn the tag so the word MISSING faces the front. Let it settle until it hangs still.
* Open the gripper and pull it straight back from the board.

**Check:** the missing slot is **flagged**. The tag hangs from the hook inside the empty outline. MISSING
faces the front and can be read from the camera. The tag does not cover the tool next to it. If the tag hangs
backwards or on the wrong hook, close on it again, lift it off, and hang it again.

**Expected state:** five tools and one tag hang on the board. No hook is empty. The tray is empty. The tag
spot is empty. The cloth is on its spot.

### Step 7: End the episode

* Check the board: five tools hang right, each inside its own outline, each one clean.
* Check the tag: it hangs on the hook inside the one empty outline, MISSING to the front.
* Check the tray: it is empty, and it stands where it started.
* Check the cloth: it lies flat on its spot.
* Check the bench and the floor: no tool, no cloth, and no tag lies on them.
* Check the base has not moved.
* Fix any failed check before you end.
* Send both arms home with the grippers open. Then stop recording.

## After the episode: reset the workspace

This reset is not recorded.

1. Take the tag off the board. Lay it flat on the tag spot, loop to the front, MISSING facing up.
2. Take all five tools off the board. Choose which tool is missing for the next episode. Put the other five in
   the tray, in one row, not touching, in any order.
3. Put a little dust on each tool in the tray, so the wipe has something to take off and the camera can see
   it.
4. Shake the dust out of the cloth. If the cloth is too dirty to wipe clean, change it. Lay a folded, dry
   cloth flat on the cloth spot.
5. Check every hook. It must be fixed, straight, and empty.
6. Check every outline. The paint must be clear enough to see the shape from the camera. Repaint any outline
   that has faded.
7. Check the tray still stands at the left end of the bench, square, and not slipping.
8. Wipe the bench dry and clear. Nothing lies in the wipe spot.
9. Change a tool if its hole or loop is broken, if it will not hang still on its hook, or if it no longer fits
   inside its outline.
10. Change the tag if the string is broken, if the word MISSING has worn off, or if it will not hang still on
    a hook.
11. Pick up anything that fell on the bench or the floor during the episode.
12. Check the base is still locked and parked square. Then do the Base positioning steps and both Setup
    checklists again.

## SOP violations

These are actions that break the SOP. They are reviewed side by side in the review tool.

### How to record a violation in review

For each violation, write down the start timestamp, the violation name, and the SOP rule broken. The visible
cue is what the reviewer sees. The coaching note is for retraining. It is not an annotation label.

### Episode handling

Tag every violation with its timestamp and its name. Keep the episode with the violation tag. Do not delete it
just because a rule was broken.

### Violations

#### Violation: Base moved during the episode

* **Visible cue:** the board moves in the frame, the bench edge changes angle in the frame, or the base rolls,
  slides, or turns at any time after recording starts.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 7. The base is parked and locked before recording and stays still for the
  whole episode.
* **Coaching note:** park it, lock it, push it to test it, then start recording. A base that moves is a lost
  episode.

#### Violation: Wrong tool taken from the tray

* **Visible cue:** a tool leaves the tray while another tool lies to the left of it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 1.1. The next tool is always the one farthest to the left.
* **Coaching note:** left to right, every time. The tray tells you the order.

#### Violation: Two tools in play at once

* **Visible cue:** a second tool leaves the tray while the first tool is still held, still being wiped, or not
  yet hanging right. Or two tools are carried in one trip.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 5. One tool leaves the tray, is wiped, and is hung before the next tool leaves the
  tray.
* **Coaching note:** one tool from the tray to the board before the next one moves.

#### Violation: Held by the wrong part

* **Visible cue:** the gripper closes on the hole or loop the tool hangs by, on the blade of the screwdriver,
  on the jaws of the pliers, or on the string of the tag. Or a tool is dragged along the tray floor instead of
  lifted.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1.2 and 6.2. Hold the tool by its handle or body, hold the tag by its body, and
  lift, do not drag.
* **Coaching note:** hold the part that is made to be held. The hole is for the hook, not for the gripper.

#### Violation: Tool not wiped

* **Visible cue:** a tool goes from the tray to the board with no wipe. Or the cloth touches one face only. Or
  the cloth is waved near the tool without going along it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 2. Every tool is wiped on both faces before it is hung.
* **Coaching note:** front face, turn it, back face. Every tool, even the one that looks clean.

#### Violation: Tool still dirty after the wipe

* **Visible cue:** dust is still plain to see on a tool when it is hung on the board.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 2.2. Wipe until the dust is gone, and wipe a dusty spot again.
* **Coaching note:** look at the tool before you lift it to the board. If you can see dust, the camera can
  too.

#### Violation: Cloth used or put back wrong

* **Visible cue:** the cloth is dragged across the bench instead of lifted, bunched into a ball, dropped on
  the floor, left in the wipe spot, left on the tray, or laid down anywhere other than the cloth spot.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2.1 and 2.3. Lift the cloth by its near edge, wipe with it flat, and lay it back
  flat on the cloth spot.
* **Coaching note:** lift it, wipe flat, lay it back in the same spot. The cloth ends where it began.

#### Violation: Something put down in the wipe spot

* **Visible cue:** a tool, the cloth, or the tag is set down on the bench in the wipe spot, even for a moment.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6. Nothing is ever put down in the wipe spot.
* **Coaching note:** the wipe spot is air, not a shelf. Hold it or hang it.

#### Violation: Hung on the wrong outline

* **Visible cue:** a tool hangs on an outline that is not its shape. For example, the spanner on the pliers
  outline, or the screwdriver on the spirit level outline. Or two tools hang on one hook.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 3 and 4. The shape decides the slot. One tool per hook.
* **Coaching note:** hold it up to the paint before you hang it. If the shape does not fit, it is the wrong
  slot.

#### Violation: Matched by position, not by shape

* **Visible cue:** tools go onto the hooks in a row from left to right, or onto the nearest empty hook each
  time, with no look at the outlines.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3.2. Go by the shape only, not by the order of the hooks.
* **Coaching note:** the hooks are not in tray order. Look at the paint, not at the row.

#### Violation: Hanging wrong

* **Visible cue:** a tool is on the right outline but hangs upside down, turned the wrong way, sticks out over
  the paint, leans on the board instead of hanging from the hook, rests on the bench under the board, or is
  still swinging when the gripper lets go.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 4.1 to 4.3. Turn the tool the way the outline shows, lower it onto the hook, and
  let it settle before you let go.
* **Coaching note:** hole at the top, same way round as the paint, wait for it to stop moving, then let go.

#### Violation: Touched a tool already on the board

* **Visible cue:** a gripper, wrist, forearm, tool, cloth, or tag knocks, brushes, lifts, or moves a tool that
  already hangs right on the board.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 4.3. Once a tool hangs right, nobody touches it again.
* **Coaching note:** hung means done. Go around it, not through it.

#### Violation: Leaned on or moved a fixture

* **Visible cue:** a gripper, wrist, or forearm rests on the board, a hook, the bench, or the tray. Or the
  tray slides or rocks. Or a hook is bent or turned.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 7. Nobody leans on, pushes, drags, or lifts the board, a hook, or the tray.
* **Coaching note:** the arm holds itself up. If something moves when you touch it, you touched it too hard.

#### Violation: Wrong slot flagged

* **Visible cue:** the tag hangs on a hook that has a tool on it, or on a hook that is not the one empty
  outline, or the tag is hung while an outline other than the missing slot is still empty.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 6.1. The tag goes on the one outline that is still empty when all five tools hang
  right.
* **Coaching note:** count the empty outlines first. One empty means flag it. Two empty means a tool is not
  hung yet.

#### Violation: Tag hung wrong or not hung

* **Visible cue:** the episode ends with the tag still on its spot, on the bench, or on the floor. Or the tag
  hangs with MISSING facing the board. Or the tag covers the tool next to it. Or the tag hangs from the
  gripper or lies on top of a hook instead of from its loop.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 6.3. Hang the tag by its loop from the hook, MISSING to the front.
* **Coaching note:** loop on the hook, word to the camera. A flag nobody can read is not a flag.

#### Violation: Tag hung before the tools

* **Visible cue:** the tag leaves its spot while a tool is still in the tray or still held.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 5 and 6. The tag is the last thing to go on the board.
* **Coaching note:** the tools tell you where the gap is. Hang all five, then flag.

#### Violation: Something dropped or picked up off the floor

* **Visible cue:** a tool, the cloth, or the tag slips out of the gripper and lands on the bench or the floor.
  Or a fallen thing is picked up again on camera.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6. One thing moves at a time, each one is put down or hung under control,
  and a fallen thing is left where it falls.
* **Coaching note:** close all the way before you lift, and let the hook have it before you let go. If it
  falls, leave it and report it.

#### Violation: Failed check not retried

* **Visible cue:** a check in a step clearly fails, and the episode carries on with no retry. For example, a
  tool hangs turned, a dusty spot is left, or the cloth lies off its spot.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6. Do the retry written under each failed check before you move on.
* **Coaching note:** a check is only worth doing if you do the retry. Fix it where it happened.

#### Violation: Wrong ending

* **Visible cue:** the episode ends with a tool still in the tray, a tool hanging wrong, the tag off the
  board, the cloth off its spot, something on the bench or the floor, an arm not home, or a gripper not fully
  open.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 7. Check the board, the tag, the tray, the cloth, the bench, the floor, and the
  base. Then send both arms home with the grippers open and stop recording.
* **Coaching note:** check first. Going home is the last thing the arms do.

## Failures that are not violations

These failures are not caused by how the task was run. Log them as system issues, throw the episode away, and
never use them for coaching.

* Recording stopped or paused during the episode (recording system).
* Camera dropped frames or lost its feed (capture system).
* Camera cannot see the outlines, the hooks, or the word on the tag, so nobody can tell if a tool is on the
  right outline or if the right slot was flagged.
* Hardware fault on an arm: gripper failure, drift, a crash caused by the controller, or a motor error.
* Base fault: a lock that will not hold, or a base that rolls, slides, or turns with the lock on.
* Faulty board: an outline that has faded so the shape cannot be seen, or a board that is loose on the wall.
* Faulty hook: one that is bent, loose, turns on its own, or will not hold a tool still.
* Faulty tool: one with a broken hole or loop, one that will not hang still on a correctly placed hook, or one
  that does not fit inside its own outline.
* Wrong tray: a tray with six tools, with four tools, with two of the same tool, or with a tool that has no
  outline on the board.
* Faulty tag: one with a broken string, one with the word worn off, or one that will not hang still.
* Faulty cloth: one that leaves fluff, one that will not take the dust off, or one that arrives already dirty.
* A place turns out to be too far for the arm with the base parked correctly, so a tool in the tray, a hook on
  the board, the cloth, or the tag cannot be reached without stretching or folding the arm.
