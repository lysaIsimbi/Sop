## Points to confirm before procurement

1. **Conveyor part count:** the SOP says the tote contains 10 parts and one part is already on the belt. Confirm whether to stage 11 total parts or take the belt misfeed from the original set of 10.
2. **Food and cleaning specifications:** the SOPs define functional properties but do not name an exact food, glass-cleaner formula, grease grade, mop-water additive, detergent-pod brand, or book sizes. Select safe, repeatable standard items and record the chosen specifications.
3. **Reusable versus replaced each episode:** define the replacement frequency for rags, cloths, mop pads, lube cards, detergent pods, food, printed parts, simulated refrigerator and pantry stock, pantry delivery boxes, and freezer label sheets.
4. **Lockout control:** the grease task requires a stopped machine with its isolator at OFF and a lockout tag already fitted. Confirm the authorized lockout procedure and responsible person before collection.
