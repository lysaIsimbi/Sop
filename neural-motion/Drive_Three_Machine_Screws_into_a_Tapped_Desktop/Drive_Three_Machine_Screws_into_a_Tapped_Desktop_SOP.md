# Drive Three Machine Screws into a Tapped Desktop SOP (1x Set: 3 Screws)

Load each machine screw onto the electric screwdriver's magnetic bit, then drive it into a
pre-tapped hole in the desktop. The screwdriver holds the screw for driving — screws are never
pre-seated in the holes by hand. Drive the three holes in order **1, 2, 3** (left to right).

## Setup

### Hardware checklist

1. Cameras are on and the full work area is visible.
2. Both arms are home with grippers open.
3. The desktop is fixed flat and stable in the center of the work area.
4. The electric screwdriver has the correct magnetic bit and is set to forward, low speed, and the
   validated clutch setting.

### Materials checklist

1. The desktop has three empty pre-tapped holes in a row.
2. Three matching machine screws are staged head-up in the holder on the left.
3. Screws, holes, and the bit are clean and undamaged; the bit holds a screw magnetically.
4. The electric screwdriver is stable in its cradle on the right.
5. The table is clear of unrelated objects.

### Hole numbering

- Left to right: **1, 2, 3**

## Vocabulary

- **Hole order:** holes are numbered **1, 2, 3** left to right, and driven in that order. A hole is
  finished only when its screw is fully seated.
- **Seated on the bit:** the screw hangs from the magnetic bit on its own — straight, head held in
  the bit, threaded end pointing down — with the left gripper open and clear.
- **Screwdriver vertical:** the bit points straight down and the tool body looks upright, not
  tilted, when viewed against the desktop.
- **Fully seated head:** the screw head sits flat against the desktop with no gap under it and no
  visible tilt.
- **Clutch activation:** the screwdriver's clutch releases and the bit stops advancing the screw.
  This is the signal to release the trigger.
- **Undamaged screw:** straight shank, intact threads, and an unmarred head that the bit engages
  without slipping.
- **Bit empty:** no screw on the magnetic bit after the tool lifts away from a driven screw.

## Steps

Drive the holes in order from hole **1** through hole **3**.

### Step 1: Pick up the screwdriver

**Goal:** hold the electric screwdriver vertical in the right gripper, bit empty and trigger untouched.

- With the **right gripper**, pick the screwdriver from its cradle without touching the trigger.
- With the **left gripper**, steady and seat the screwdriver in the right gripper so the bit points
  straight down and the screwdriver is vertical; release the left gripper once it is seated.
- Hold the screwdriver in the right gripper for the whole set. Do not re-cradle it between screws.

### Step 2: Load one screw onto the bit

**Goal:** transfer one undamaged screw from the holder onto the magnetic bit, head seated in the bit.

#### 2.1 Pick one screw

- With the **left gripper**, grasp one screw by the sides of its head, threaded end pointing down.
- Confirm the screw is straight and undamaged.

#### 2.2 Present the head to the bit

- Raise the screw so its head faces the screwdriver's magnetic bit; keep the threaded end down.
- Bring the bit and the screw head together until the magnet holds the screw and the head is seated
  in the bit.

#### 2.3 Release the screw

- Open the **left gripper** only when the bit is holding the screw.

**Check:** the screw hangs from the bit — straight, head seated in the bit, threaded end pointing
down. If it is crooked or drops, reload it once. If it still will not seat, end the episode.

### Step 3: Drive the screw

**Goal:** drive the loaded screw straight until its head is fully seated against the desktop.

#### 3.1 Align over the hole

- Move the screwdriver so the threaded tip enters the current hole; keep the screwdriver vertical.
- With the **left gripper**, steady the desktop at the marked holding area if it can shift.

#### 3.2 Drive

- Press the trigger at low speed and drive the screw into the pre-tapped hole.
- Release the trigger immediately when the clutch activates.

#### 3.3 Lift away

- Lift the screwdriver straight up so the bit separates from the seated screw head.

**Check:** the screw is straight, its head is fully seated against the desktop, and the head is not
damaged; the bit is now empty. If the screw tilts, stops moving downward, or the bit slips, release
the trigger and end the episode. Do not force the screw.

### Step 4: Install the remaining screws

**Goal:** install one fully seated screw in each of the three holes in numerical order.

- Repeat Steps 2–3 for holes 2 and 3, keeping the screwdriver in the right gripper throughout.
- Complete one screw before loading the next screw.
- Follow hole order **1, 2, 3**.

### Step 5: Finish the episode

**Goal:** finish with three correctly installed screws, the tool cradled, and both arms home.

- Confirm there are three straight, fully seated screws, one in each hole.
- Confirm the screw holder is empty and the bit is empty.
- Return the screwdriver to its cradle.
- Return both arms home with grippers open, then end recording.

## After the episode: reset the workspace

Reset with recording off.

1. Set the screwdriver to reverse and remove screws 3 through 1.
2. Inspect the screws, holes, and bit. Replace damaged parts.
3. Return the screwdriver to forward, low speed, and the validated clutch setting.
4. Stage three screws head-up in the holder and leave all desktop holes empty.
5. Run the Setup checklist before the next episode.

## SOP violations

These are actions that break the SOP and are reviewed in the side-by-side review tool.

### How to record a violation in review

For every violation, record:

- The **start timestamp** of the violation in the video.
- The **violation name** from the list below.
- The **SOP rule broken**.

The visible cue is what the reviewer sees in the video. The coaching note is for retraining and is
not an annotation label.

### Episode handling

Tag every violation with its timestamp and name. Retain the episode in training data with
the violation tag. Do not delete it solely because of a violation.

### Violations

**Violation: Wrong installation order**

- **Visible cue:** a screw is driven into a higher-numbered hole while a lower-numbered hole is
  empty or unfinished.
- **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1–4 (drive and finish the holes in order 1, 2, 3).
- **Coaching note:** finish the current numbered hole before moving to the next one.

**Violation: Screwdriver not seated squarely in the right gripper**

- **Visible cue:** the screwdriver sits tilted or loose in the right gripper and the left gripper
  does not square it before the first screw is loaded, so the bit is not vertical when driving
  begins.
- **SOP rule broken:** Step 1 (use the left gripper to seat the screwdriver vertical in the right
  gripper before loading a screw).
- **Coaching note:** square the screwdriver in the right gripper with the left gripper before
  loading the first screw.

**Violation: Screw not loaded on the bit before driving**

- **Visible cue:** the screwdriver moves toward a hole while the left gripper still holds the screw;
  the screw is crooked, loose, or falling off the bit; or the bit is empty on approach.
- **SOP rule broken:** Step 2 (load the screw onto the magnetic bit, release the gripper, and
  confirm it is seated straight before driving).
- **Coaching note:** seat the screw on the bit and release the gripper before moving to the hole.

**Violation: Misaligned screw forced**

- **Visible cue:** the screw tilts, wobbles, binds, or stops moving downward and the electric
  screwdriver keeps running.
- **SOP rule broken:** Step 3.2 (stop immediately if the screw tilts, stops advancing, or the bit
  slips; never force the screw).
- **Coaching note:** release the trigger at the first sign of misalignment or binding.

**Violation: Screwdriver used incorrectly**

- **Visible cue:** the bit rotates before the screw tip is in the hole, the screwdriver is not
  vertical, the bit slips and continues rotating, or the trigger remains pressed after the clutch
  activates.
- **SOP rule broken:** Steps 3.1–3.2 (enter the hole with the screw tip, keep the screwdriver
  vertical, and release the trigger immediately when the clutch activates).
- **Coaching note:** align first, drive vertically at low speed, and stop at the clutch signal.

**Violation: Incorrect final screw position**

- **Visible cue:** the next screw is started while the current screw is tilted, loose, damaged, or
  its head is not fully seated against the desktop.
- **SOP rule broken:** Steps 3.3–4 (confirm each screw is straight, undamaged, and fully seated
  before loading the next screw).
- **Coaching note:** inspect and finish one screw before moving to the next hole.

**Violation: Incorrect episode ending**

- **Visible cue:** the episode ends without three straight and seated screws, with a screw remaining
  in the holder, with the screwdriver outside its cradle, with an arm away from home or a gripper
  closed, or before the 3-second final hold is complete.
- **SOP rule broken:** Step 5 (verify all three screws, clear the input, cradle the tool, home both
  arms with grippers open, and then end recording).
- **Coaching note:** complete the final verification and safe-home sequence before ending.

### Non-violation failures

Failures that are not caused by how the task was run do not go in the violation set. Log them as system
issues, discard the episode, and do not use them for coaching.

- **Recording or camera failure:** recording stops, frames drop, or a camera feed is lost.
- **Hardware failure:** the robot, gripper, electric screwdriver, magnetic bit, clutch, or desktop
  fixture fails.
- **Latent material defect:** a hidden screw or tapped-hole defect prevents correct installation.

## Annotation subtasks (from SOP)

1. Pick up the electric screwdriver
2. Load one screw onto the magnetic bit
3. Align the loaded screwdriver over the hole
4. Drive and seat the screw
5. Verify the desktop and end the episode
