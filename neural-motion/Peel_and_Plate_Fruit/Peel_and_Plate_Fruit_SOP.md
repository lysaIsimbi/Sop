# Peel and Plate Fruit SOP

One episode peels one banana and one orange, breaks both into segments on one plate, arranges the
segments into two rows, throws the peels away, wipes the working area, and moves the finished plate to
the serving area.

The order never changes: plate first, then the banana, then the orange, then segment both, then arrange,
then discard the peels, then wipe, then serve. Nothing is segmented until every peel strip is off the
flesh, the peels are not thrown away until the arranging is finished, and the plate does not leave the
working area for the serving area until the table under it has been wiped.

The left gripper carries the plate and holds each fruit and each peel down against the table while the
right gripper works on it. The right gripper takes the fruit, splits, scores, peels, breaks the
segments, arranges them, throws the peels away, and wipes. Neither gripper crosses to the other side of
the table.

Every fruit is peeled lying on the table, never held up in the air, and an orange is turned by spinning
it on the table rather than in a gripper. Peeled fruit and fruit segments rest on the plate face and
nowhere else. Nothing edible ever touches the table.

## Setup

Complete both checklists before starting an episode.

### Hardware checklist

1. Cameras are on and recording.
2. The environment camera shows the whole table: the fruit spot at the back right, the peel rest
   right of the working area, the cloth rest at the front right, the waste bin at the front right
   corner, the plate stack at the front left, the plate rest left of the working area, and the
   serving area at the back left.
3. The working area at the center of the table is visible from above, so every peel strip can be
   seen coming off and any juice or fruit left on the table can be seen.
4. Both arms are at home with grippers open.
5. The table is clean, dry, and bare apart from the fruit, the plates, the cloth, and the waste bin.
6. The left arm reaches the plate stack, the working area, the plate rest, and the serving area. The
   right arm reaches the fruit spot, the working area, the peel rest, the cloth rest, and the waste
   bin.

### Materials checklist

1. One whole **banana** and one whole **orange** lie at the fruit spot at the back right, the banana
   in front of the orange.
2. The banana is ripe and unsplit with its **banana stem** intact, firm enough that the flesh does
   not break apart under a correct peel.
3. The orange is whole and unscored with its peel intact, and its **orange crown** is up.
4. Clean dry **plates** are stacked face up in the plate stack at the front left. One plate is used
   per episode, and every plate is the same size.
5. One folded dry **cloth** per episode lies on the cloth rest at the front right. A cloth is used
   for one episode and thrown away.
6. One open **waste bin**, empty, at the front right corner.
7. The working area at the center of the table, the peel rest right of it, the plate rest left of
   it, and the serving area at the back left are all clear.

### Workspace layout

* **Fruit spot:** back right, the whole banana and the whole orange.
* **Peel rest:** right of the working area, where the banana peel and the orange peel strips wait
  until they are thrown away.
* **Cloth rest:** front right, the folded cloth.
* **Waste bin:** front right corner, open and empty.
* **Working area:** the center of the table, where the peeling, segmenting, and arranging happen.
* **Plate stack:** front left, the clean plates stacked face up.
* **Plate rest:** left of the working area, where the arranged plate waits during the wipe.
* **Serving area:** back left, where the finished plate ends the episode.

Everything the right arm works on is staged on the right or in the center. The plate, the only thing the
left arm carries, moves along the left. No carried thing crosses the midline.

## Vocabulary

* **Arm assignment:** the left gripper carries the plate and holds a fruit or a peel down against
  the table or the plate while the right gripper works on it. The right gripper takes the fruit,
  splits, scores, pulls the peel strips, breaks the segments, arranges them, throws the peels away,
  and wipes. Neither gripper takes the other's zones.
* **Banana stem:** the short stalk at the narrow end of the banana, where the skin is split. The
  **banana tip** is the blunt end opposite it.
* **Banana skin:** the outer covering of the banana, taken off as three peel strips. The **banana
  peel** is all of that skin once it is off the flesh, still joined at the banana tip.
* **Orange crown:** the small flat scar where the stalk was, at the top of the orange. It is the
  start point for every score line.
* **Score line:** a break through the orange peel only, running from the orange crown to the
  opposite end, that does not reach the orange flesh.
* **Peel strip:** one length of banana skin or orange peel pulled away from the flesh in one motion.
* **Peeled fruit:** banana flesh or orange flesh with no skin and no peel strip left on it.
* **Fruit segment:** one banana segment or one orange segment. Banana segments are broken to an even
  size, four from one banana. Orange segments are separated whole along their own dividing lines.
* **Plate:** one clean plate that receives the fruit. Its **plate rim** is the raised outer edge and
  the only part a gripper holds. Its **plate face** is the flat inner surface the segments sit on.
* **Near half:** the half of the plate face closest to the front edge, holding the banana segments.
  The **far half** is the half farthest from the front edge, holding the orange segments.
* **Arranged plate:** all banana segments in one row on the near half and all orange segments in one
  row on the far half, none stacked, none touching the plate rim.
* **Cloth:** the folded cloth used to wipe the working area once, then thrown away. Its **cloth
  face** is the flat side that touches the table.
* **Wipe pass:** one continuous draw of the cloth face from left to right across the working area.
* **Table hold:** the left gripper closed on a fruit, a peel, or a piece of flesh and holding it
  down against the table or the plate face while the right gripper works on it. The thing being held
  never leaves the surface.
* **Spin in place:** turning the orange a quarter turn about its own upright axis by pressing its
  side with a gripper while it stays on the table, orange crown up. An orange is never turned in a
  gripper.
* **Lift:** coming straight down onto the grasp point, closing, and raising straight up until there
  is daylight under the thing, before anything moves sideways.
* **Release point:** over the plate face, over the peel rest, over the working area, over the plate
  rest, over the serving area, or over the waste bin. A gripper opens nowhere else.
* **Debris:** any fruit segment, peel strip, juice, or pulp outside a plate, the peel rest, or the
  waste bin.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 9 are one plate. Step 10 ends the episode.

### Step 1: Move a plate to the working area

**Goal:** one clean plate lying flat in the working area, plate face up, ready to receive fruit.

* The **left gripper** closes on the **plate rim** of the top plate in the plate stack, lifts it
  straight up, carries it level to the **working area**, lowers it until it lies flat with the plate
  face up, centered in the working area, and releases.

Take one plate. Never lift two plates in one grasp, and never drag a plate across the table.

**Check:** one plate lies flat and centered in the working area, plate face up, with clear table on both
sides of it.

### Step 2: Peel the banana

**Goal:** the banana flesh lying on the near half of the plate as peeled fruit, and the banana peel on
the peel rest.

#### 2.1 Move the banana to the working area

* The **right gripper** closes on the **banana** around the middle of its length at the fruit spot,
  lifts it straight up, carries it level to the working area, lays it flat beside the plate on the
  plate's right with the banana stem pointing toward the front edge, and releases.

#### 2.2 Split the banana skin

* The **left gripper** takes a **table hold** on the banana at the **banana tip**.
* The **right gripper** closes on the **banana stem** and bends it down toward the table until the
  banana skin splits open at the banana stem.
* If the skin does not split in one bend, release, take the banana stem again, and bend a second
  time.

#### 2.3 Pull the peel strips

* The **right gripper** closes on one split edge of the banana skin at the banana stem and pulls it
  down the length of the banana toward the banana tip in one motion, laying it open flat on the
  table.
* Repeat with the next split edge until three peel strips lie open around the flesh and no skin
  covers the **banana flesh**.
* If a peel strip tears before it reaches the banana tip, take the torn end once and pull it on to
  the banana tip.
* The **left gripper** keeps its table hold on the banana tip through the whole of this.

#### 2.4 Move the banana flesh to the plate

* The **right gripper** closes on the **banana flesh** just below the banana stem end, lifts it
  straight up clear of the open peel, carries it level to the **near half** of the plate, lowers it
  onto the plate face, and releases.
* The **left gripper** keeps its table hold on the peel so the flesh comes free of it, then
  releases.

#### 2.5 Set the banana peel on the peel rest

* The **right gripper** closes on the **banana peel** at the banana tip, lifts it, carries it to the
  **peel rest**, lowers it, and releases.

The banana stays flat on the table for the whole peel. Never lift the banana to split or peel it, and
never let the flesh touch the table.

**Check:** the banana flesh lies on the near half of the plate with no banana skin and no peel strip
left on it, the banana peel is on the peel rest, and the table is clear of debris.

### Step 3: Peel the orange

**Goal:** the orange flesh lying on the far half of the plate as peeled fruit, and every orange peel
strip on the peel rest.

#### 3.1 Move the orange to the working area

* The **right gripper** closes on the **orange** around its middle at the fruit spot, lifts it
  straight up, carries it level to the working area, lowers it beside the plate on the plate's right
  with the **orange crown** up, and releases.

#### 3.2 Score the orange peel

* The **left gripper** takes a **table hold** on the orange, orange crown up.
* The **right gripper** presses into the orange peel at the orange crown until the peel opens and
  the orange flesh below is not broken.
* Draw one **score line** from the orange crown down to the opposite end.
* The **left gripper** releases, the **right gripper** does a **spin in place** of a quarter turn,
  the left gripper takes its table hold again, and the next score line is drawn.
* Repeat until four score lines divide the orange peel into four even parts.

#### 3.3 Pull the peel strips

* The **left gripper** takes a **table hold** on the orange.
* The **right gripper** closes on the edge of one part of the orange peel at the orange crown and
  pulls it away from the **orange flesh**, from the orange crown toward the opposite end, in one
  motion so it comes off as one peel strip.
* Carry the peel strip to the **peel rest**, lower it, and release.
* Repeat for each remaining part until no orange peel covers the orange flesh.
* If a peel strip tears before it reaches the opposite end, take the torn end once and pull it clear
  of the orange flesh.

#### 3.4 Move the orange flesh to the plate

* The **right gripper** closes on the **orange flesh**, lifts it straight up, carries it level to
  the **far half** of the plate, lowers it onto the plate face, and releases.
* The **left gripper** releases.

The orange stays on the table for the whole peel, and it is turned only by spinning it in place. Never
lift the orange to score it and never turn it in a gripper.

**Check:** the orange flesh lies on the far half of the plate with no orange peel left on it, every
orange peel strip is on the peel rest, the orange flesh is not broken open, and the table is clear of
debris and juice.

### Step 4: Segment the banana onto the plate

**Goal:** the banana flesh broken into four even banana segments, all on the near half of the plate.

* The **left gripper** takes a **table hold** on the **banana flesh** at the banana tip end, against
  the plate face.
* The **right gripper** closes on the banana flesh a short way in from the banana stem end and lifts
  that end away until one **banana segment** breaks off.
* Lower the banana segment onto the **near half** of the plate and release.
* Repeat until the banana flesh is four even banana segments, then release both grippers.

Break each segment by lifting one end away, not by closing the gripper harder. Every break happens over
the plate face so nothing edible reaches the table.

**Check:** four even banana segments sit on the near half of the plate, none crushed, split along their
length, or left as pulp on a gripper, and no banana is on the table.

### Step 5: Segment the orange onto the plate

**Goal:** the orange flesh separated into whole orange segments, all on the far half of the plate.

* The **left gripper** takes a **table hold** on the **orange flesh** against the plate face.
* The **right gripper** closes on one **orange segment** at its outer edge and pulls it away along
  its own dividing line until it separates whole.
* Lower it onto the **far half** of the plate and release.
* Repeat until every orange segment is separated and none are left joined, then release both
  grippers.

Find the dividing line before pulling. A segment torn open across its middle counts as a failed
separation.

**Check:** every orange segment sits whole on the far half of the plate, none torn open, none left
joined to another, and no orange is on the table.

### Step 6: Arrange the fruit on the plate

**Goal:** an arranged plate, two rows of fruit segments with none stacked and none touching the plate
rim.

* The **right gripper** closes on one **banana segment** and places it on the **near half** in one
  row running left to right, beside the segment next to it and clear of the plate rim.
* Repeat until all four banana segments sit in one row on the near half.
* The **right gripper** then places each **orange segment** the same way in one row on the **far
  half**, each beside the one next to it and clear of the plate rim.
* Release once every segment is resting.

No segment sits on top of another, hangs over the plate rim, or leaves a visible gap in its row.

**Check:** four banana segments sit in one row on the near half, every orange segment sits in one row on
the far half, none are stacked, none touch or overhang the plate rim, and each row reads as a row with
no gap.

### Step 7: Discard the peels

**Goal:** the peel rest empty and every peel in the waste bin.

* The **right gripper** closes on the **banana peel** at the banana tip on the peel rest, lifts it,
  carries it to the **waste bin**, and releases it over the bin.
* The **right gripper** then takes each **orange peel strip** from the peel rest the same way and
  releases it over the waste bin.
* Repeat until the peel rest holds no banana peel and no orange peel strip.

**Check:** the peel rest is empty, the waste bin holds the banana peel and every orange peel strip, and
no peel is on the table, on the plate, or outside the bin.

### Step 8: Wipe the working area

**Goal:** the working area clean and dry, with no debris and no juice on the surface.

#### 8.1 Move the arranged plate clear

* The **left gripper** closes on the **plate rim** of the arranged plate, lifts it level, carries it
  to the **plate rest**, lowers it until it lies flat, and releases.

#### 8.2 Pick up the cloth

* The **right gripper** closes on the folded **cloth** on the cloth rest, carries it to the far edge
  of the working area, and lowers it until the **cloth face** lies flat on the table.

#### 8.3 Wipe

* Draw the cloth from left to right in one continuous **wipe pass**, keeping the cloth face flat on
  the table for the whole pass.
* Lift at the end of the pass, return to the left side, and start the next wipe pass nearer the
  front edge so it overlaps the pass just made.
* Repeat, working from the far edge to the near edge, until the last wipe pass reaches the near edge
  of the working area.
* If any debris or juice is left after the last wipe pass, run one short repeat pass over that spot.

#### 8.4 Discard the cloth

* The **right gripper** carries the cloth to the **waste bin** and releases it over the bin.

Every wipe pass runs left to right and parallel to the front edge. The plate is out of the working area
before the cloth comes in, so the cloth never passes over the arranged plate.

**Check:** the working area is clean and dry with no debris and no juice, the cloth is in the waste bin,
and the arranged plate is on the plate rest with both rows intact.

### Step 9: Move the plate to the serving area

**Goal:** the arranged plate resting flat in the serving area with both rows intact.

* The **left gripper** closes on the **plate rim**, lifts the plate level, carries it level to the
  **serving area**, lowers it until it lies flat and centered in the serving area with the banana
  segment row nearest the front edge, and releases.

Keep the plate level for the whole carry. Never tilt it and never let a segment slide out of its row.

**Check:** the plate lies flat and centered in the serving area, the banana row is nearest the front edge
and the orange row farthest, and no segment has changed rows, shifted onto another, or fallen off.

### Step 10: Confirm the plate and end the episode

* Confirm the serving area holds one arranged plate with both rows intact, the working area is clean
  and dry, the peel rest is empty, the fruit spot is empty, and the waste bin holds both peels and
  the cloth.
* Return both arms home, clear of the serving area and the working area, then stop recording.

## After the episode: reset the workspace

This reset is not recorded.

1. Take the arranged plate off the serving area and tip the fruit segments into the waste bin.
2. Wash and dry the plate, then stack it face up in the plate stack at the front left.
3. Lay one whole banana and one whole orange at the fruit spot at the back right, banana in front of
   orange, with the orange crown up.
4. Lay one folded dry cloth on the cloth rest at the front right.
5. Empty the waste bin and set it back at the front right corner, open.
6. Wipe any debris and juice off the table and confirm the surface is clean and dry. A wet table
   lets the plate and the orange slide under a table hold.
7. Confirm the working area, the peel rest, the plate rest, and the serving area are clear.
8. Replace a banana that is bruised, split, or so ripe that the flesh breaks apart, and an orange
   that is dry, mouldy, or already split.

## SOP violations

These are actions that break the SOP and are reviewed side by side in the review tool.

### How to record a violation in review

For each violation, record the **start timestamp**, **violation name**, and **SOP rule broken**. The
visible cue is what the reviewer sees. The coaching note is for retraining and is not an annotation
label.

### Episode handling

Tag every violation with its timestamp and name. Keep the episode with the violation tag. Do
not delete it just because a rule was broken.

### Violations

**Violation: Wrong order**

* **Visible cue:** the orange is peeled before the banana, a fruit is segmented before both fruits
  are peeled or while skin or peel is still on the flesh, the peels go in the bin before the
  arranging is finished, the working area is wiped before the peels are thrown away, or the plate
  goes to the serving area before the wipe.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 9, the order never changes and the flesh is fully peeled before
  any segmenting.
* **Coaching note:** plate, banana, orange, segment, arrange, discard, wipe, serve. Check the flesh
  for leftover skin before you break it.

**Violation: Arms swapped roles**

* **Visible cue:** the right gripper carries the plate, or the left gripper takes fruit from the
  fruit spot, pulls a peel strip, breaks a segment, arranges a row, takes the cloth, or reaches the
  waste bin.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 9, the left gripper carries the plate and holds, and the right
  gripper does the fruit, the peels, the segments, the arranging, and the wipe.
* **Coaching note:** each arm stays on its own side. Nothing crosses the midline.

**Violation: Fruit worked off the table**

* **Visible cue:** the banana leaves the table while it is split or peeled, the orange leaves the
  table while it is scored or peeled, or the orange is lifted or twisted in a gripper to turn it
  between score lines instead of being spun in place.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2, 3, and 3.2, every fruit is peeled lying on the table and the orange
  is turned by spinning it in place with the orange crown up.
* **Coaching note:** hold it down and work against the table. To turn the orange, let go, spin it on
  the table, and take the hold again.

**Violation: Fruit not held during a split, score, or pull**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper is off the fruit while the banana stem is bent, while a score
  line is drawn, or while a peel strip is pulled; or the fruit slides, rolls, or lifts off the table
  during any of them.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2.2, 2.3, 3.2, and 3.3, the left gripper holds the fruit down against
  the table for the whole of each.
* **Coaching note:** the table hold goes on before the right gripper starts and comes off after it
  finishes.

**Violation: Banana skin split or a peel strip pulled wrong**

* **Visible cue:** the banana skin opens along the side of the banana or at the banana tip, or the
  flesh is squeezed out through the skin, instead of splitting at the banana stem; or a banana peel
  strip is pulled from the banana tip toward the banana stem, or an orange peel strip from the
  opposite end toward the orange crown.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2.2, 2.3, and 3.3, the skin splits at the banana stem and every peel
  strip is pulled from the split or scored end toward the opposite end.
* **Coaching note:** the split starts at the stem only, and every pull starts at the banana stem or
  the orange crown and works away from it.

**Violation: Orange scored wrong**

* **Visible cue:** juice runs from a score line or the orange flesh is visibly broken open along one
  before any peel strip is pulled, fewer or more than four score lines are drawn, or the orange is
  not spun a quarter turn between them so the parts of the peel are uneven.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3.2, four score lines a quarter turn apart divide the orange peel into
  four even parts, and a score line goes through the orange peel only.
* **Coaching note:** press only until the peel opens, spin a quarter turn between lines, and draw
  exactly four.

**Violation: Peeled fruit or a fruit segment off the plate face**

* **Visible cue:** the banana flesh, the orange flesh, a banana segment, or an orange segment is set
  down on the table, on the peel rest, or on the plate rim; or a banana segment is placed on the far
  half, or an orange segment on the near half.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2.4, 3.4, 4, 5, and 6, peeled fruit and fruit segments rest on the
  plate face and nowhere else, banana on the near half and orange on the far half.
* **Coaching note:** flesh goes straight onto the plate, banana row near the front edge and orange
  row away from it.

**Violation: Segments not broken cleanly**

* **Visible cue:** at the end of Step 4 the banana flesh is in fewer or more than four segments or
  the segments are visibly uneven, the banana flesh flattens, splits along its length, or leaves
  pulp on the gripper, or at the end of Step 5 an orange segment is torn open across its middle or
  left joined to another.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 4 and 5, the banana breaks into four even banana segments by lifting
  one end away, and every orange segment separates whole along its own dividing line.
* **Coaching note:** take the same distance in from the end each time and lift the end away rather
  than squeezing. On the orange, find the dividing line first, then pull.

**Violation: Rows not arranged**

* **Visible cue:** at the end of Step 6 a fruit segment sits on top of another, touches or overhangs
  the plate rim, or a row has a visible gap in it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 6, one row per half, each segment beside the one next to it, clear of
  the plate rim, and none stacked.
* **Coaching note:** set each segment down beside its neighbor, not on it, and keep the whole row
  clear of the rim.

**Violation: Peel left on the peel rest or the table**

* **Visible cue:** at the end of Step 7 the banana peel or any orange peel strip is still on the
  peel rest, on the table, or on the plate, or a peel is dropped outside the waste bin.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 7, the peel rest is empty and every peel is in the waste bin.
* **Coaching note:** clear every peel strip off the rest and release each one over the bin.

**Violation: Plate wiped around instead of moved clear**

* **Visible cue:** the cloth is drawn around or over the arranged plate while it is still in the
  working area, or the plate is left close enough that the cloth passes over it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 8.1, the arranged plate goes to the plate rest before the cloth comes
  in.
* **Coaching note:** move the plate out first. A cloth near an arranged plate breaks the rows.

**Violation: Wipe skipped, misdirected, or the working area left dirty**

* **Visible cue:** Step 8 is skipped, debris or juice is still on the working area at the end of it
  and the episode moves on without a repeat pass, the wipe passes run right to left, front to back,
  or on the diagonal, or a strip of the working area is left untouched between two passes.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 8 and 8.3, left to right wipe passes from the far edge to the near
  edge, each overlapping the pass just made, until the working area is clean and dry.
* **Coaching note:** keep every pass parallel to the front edge, work far to near, overlap the last
  one, then check and run a short repeat pass over any spot still dirty.

**Violation: Plate set down wrong or a row broken in a carry**

* **Visible cue:** a fruit segment slides out of its row, changes halves, or falls off the plate
  while the plate is carried in Step 8.1 or Step 9; or the plate is released outside the serving
  area, left in the working area or on the plate rest at the end of the episode, or set down with
  the orange row nearest the front edge.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 8.1 and 9, the plate is carried level and lies flat and centered in the
  serving area with the banana row nearest the front edge and both rows intact.
* **Coaching note:** keep the plate level through the whole carry and keep the banana row facing
  you. If a row breaks, arrange it again per Step 6 before going on.

**Violation: Bad pick, carry, or release**

* **Visible cue:** two plates are lifted from the plate stack in one grasp, the banana and the
  orange are lifted together, a thing stays in contact with the table while it moves between two
  zones, a gripper shifts or seats its hold again in the air, or a gripper opens over the table or
  over open space instead of over the spot the thing is going to.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 9, one plate, one banana, and one orange per episode, each lifted
  clear of the table before it moves, held without a new grip in the air, and released over its
  destination.
* **Coaching note:** one thing per pick, lift before you carry, set it down before you take a new
  hold, and know where it lands before you open the gripper.

**Violation: Placement shoved or fiddled with**

* **Visible cue:** a gripper nudges, pats, or slides the plate or a fruit segment into position
  after releasing it instead of lifting it clear and placing it again, or makes more than about two
  small corrections to settle a placement or a row.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1, 6, 8.1, and 9, correct a bad placement by lifting the thing clear
  and placing it again, and each placement settles in one or two small corrections.
* **Coaching note:** lift it and lay it again rather than shoving it. If the first placement lands
  wide every time, tighten the approach rather than correcting after.

**Violation: Dropped, collided, or knocked something over**

* **Visible cue:** the plate, a fruit, a peel, or a segment falls to the table or the floor short of
  its spot, the waste bin is struck and shifts, a row is swept off the plate, or the two arms strike
  each other.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 9, keep each thing on a clear travel path and release only after a
  settled placement.
* **Coaching note:** check the path and the landing spot before you move.

**Violation: Wrong episode ending**

* **Visible cue:** the episode ends with the plate off the serving area, a row broken, a peel on the
  peel rest, fruit or juice on the working area, the cloth on the table, an arm away from home, an
  arm still over the plate.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 10, confirm the plate and the clear zones; return both arms home clear
  of the scene; then stop recording.
* **Coaching note:** confirm first. Homing is the last thing the arms do.

### Failures that are not violations

These failures are not caused by how the task was run. Log them as system issues, discard the episode, and never
use them for coaching.

* **Recording stopped or paused during the episode** (recording system).
* **Camera dropped frames or lost its feed** (capture system).
* **Hardware fault on an arm:** gripper failure, drift, controller caused collision, or motor error.
* **Defective banana:** bruised, split, or so ripe that the flesh breaks apart under a correct peel,
  or a banana stem that snaps off instead of splitting the skin. Replace it before the next episode.
* **Defective orange:** dry, mouldy, or already split, or a peel that will not come away as a peel
  strip under a correct pull. Replace it before the next episode.
* **Defective plate:** cracked, wet, or dirty when the episode begins. Replace it before the next
  episode.
* **Defective cloth:** already wet, torn, or shedding fibre onto the table. Replace it before the
  next episode.
* **Fruit or plate slides** under a correct table hold because the table is wet or slick. Wipe and
  dry the table before the next episode.

## Annotation subtasks (from SOP)

1. Move one plate from the plate stack to the working area
2. Peel the banana and place the banana flesh on the plate
