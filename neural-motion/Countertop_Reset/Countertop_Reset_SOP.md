# Countertop Reset SOP (3x Items)

One episode is one full reset of a three item counter. It runs from three items standing out of line
on a dirty counter, to a clean counter with the same three items back in their own spots, standing in
a neat row along the front table edge.

The two grippers have fixed roles and never swap. The **left gripper is the item gripper**: it lifts
items off the counter, carries them back to the staging strip, brings them forward again, and
straightens them. The **right gripper is the cloth gripper**: it takes the cloth, wipes the counter,
and parks the cloth. The left gripper never touches the cloth. The right gripper never touches an
item. Nothing is ever passed from one gripper to the other, and only one arm works at a time.

The order never changes: **move all three items back to the staging strip, take the cloth, wipe the
counter, park the cloth, bring the three items forward again, then straighten the row.** The counter
is wiped only when it is empty. No item comes forward until the whole counter is clean and the cloth
is parked.

One of the three items is the appliance and it is the only one with a handle. The appliance is always
lifted by its handle, never by its body. The other two items have no handle and are lifted by the
body. Never move more than one item per trip. Never drag or slide an item: every item is lifted clear of the table and
carried. The only movement on the table is a small nudge while the row is straightened at the end.
Never turn an item in the air. Never let an item hang over the front or the back table edge. Never
set the cloth down anywhere but its park. Never change the left to right order of the three items.

## Setup

Complete both checklists before starting an episode.

### Hardware checklist

1. Cameras are on and recording.
2. The environment camera is centered on the counter and its frame includes the front table edge and
   the back table edge along the full run, the whole counter, the whole staging strip, and the cloth
   park.
3. The dirt on the counter is visible on camera before the wipe.
4. Both arms are at home with grippers open.
5. The table holds nothing but the three items and the cloth.
6. The left arm reaches all three items on the counter and all three spots on the staging strip
   without extending to a joint limit and without reaching out past the back table edge. The right
   arm reaches both ends of the counter and the cloth park the same way. Make the run narrower until
   both are true.
7. Each arm reaches the counter and the staging strip from above and from the front.
8. Arm paths along the counter, back to the staging strip, and out to the cloth park are clear, and
   each arm can cross the table midline without hitting anything.

### Materials checklist

1. All three items stand on the counter in one left to right row, each one on its own spot.
2. Every item is visibly out of line: pushed back or sideways, with uneven gaps to its neighbours.
   The mess is obvious at a glance.
3. Every item stands upright on its own, is empty and dry so nothing spills when it is lifted, and
   can be lifted by one gripper.
4. The appliance has a handle that is firm on it and open enough for the gripper to close on. The
   other two items have no handle and can be gripped by the body.
5. The counter carries visible dirt, including dirt around and between the items.
6. The staging strip is empty.
7. The folded cloth lies flat and damp on the cloth park.
8. The cloth park holds nothing but the cloth, and the table holds nothing else at all.

### Workspace layout

There is no wall behind the station and nothing on the table is marked or taped out. Every area below
is a piece of the bare tabletop, judged by eye against the table edges and the items themselves. From
the front of the table going back: the front table edge, the counter, the staging strip, the back
table edge.

* **Front table edge:** the near edge of the table. The finished row is lined up against it. Nothing
  may hang over it.
* **Counter:** the strip of table just inside the front table edge where the three items stand. It is
  far enough in from that edge that no item hangs over. It is dirty and full at episode start, and
  clean and full at episode end.
* **Staging strip:** the clear strip of table behind the counter. It holds the items while the
  counter is wiped. It is empty at episode start and empty again at episode end.
* **Back table edge:** the far edge of the table, behind the staging strip. The staging strip sits far
  enough in from it that no item hangs over. No arm reaches out past this edge.
* **Cloth park:** the area out to the right of the counter where the folded cloth rests when it is not
  in use. Worked by the right gripper only.
* **Arm home:** the resting pose for each arm, gripper open and clear of the table.

## Vocabulary

* **Arm assignment:** fixed by object, not by where it lies. The **left gripper** moves and
  straightens items and works the counter and the staging strip. The **right gripper** handles the
  cloth and works the counter and the cloth park. No object is ever passed from one gripper to the
  other.
* **Idle arm:** the arm not named in a step. An empty idle arm waits at home, clear of the table. It
  never steadies an item, holds the cloth flat, or helps the working arm.
* **Item:** one of the three objects in the row. Each one stands upright on its own. One item is the
  **appliance**. The other two have no handle.
* **Handle:** the grip on the appliance, and the only part of the appliance the left gripper ever
  closes on. The other two items have no handle, so the gripper closes on the body instead. Either
  way the gripper comes down from above.
* **Row:** the three items standing on the counter together.
* **Start spot:** the piece of counter one item stood on at episode start. Each item goes back to its
  own start spot, so the left to right order never changes.
* **Neat row:** every item stands on the counter with a narrow gap between it and the front table
  edge, and that gap looks the same for every item. No two items touch, and the gaps between
  neighbours look the same along the row. No item hangs over the front table edge or over the back of
  the counter.
* **Cloth:** the damp folded cloth used on the counter. It is held so a broad flat side of it meets
  the table, never by a corner and never balled up.
* **Pass:** one draw of the cloth from the left end of the counter to the right end, touching the
  table the whole way.
* **Dirt:** any mark, dust ring, crumb, smear, or leftover residue you can see on the counter.
* **Release point:** the gripper opens only over the staging strip, over a clean counter, or over the
  cloth park. Never over another item and never over open table.
* **Stable placement:** the item stands still after the gripper opens, sits fully on the counter or
  the staging strip, and does not rock, lean on a neighbour, or slide.

## Steps

### Step 1: Move the three items back to the staging strip

**Goal:** the counter is empty and all three items stand on the staging strip.

Work left to right, one item per trip. Finish one item before starting the next.

* The **left gripper** comes down on the item from above and closes on it, so the item hangs upright
  and not tilted. Close on the handle for the appliance, and on the body for the other two items.
* If the grip is not solid before the lift, open and try again. Two tries at most. If the item still
  will not hold, end the episode and check the item in the reset.
* Lift the item straight up clear of the counter, carry it back, and set it down on the staging strip
  straight behind the spot it came from.
* Do not turn the item as you carry it. The appliance handle points the same way when it lands as it
  did when it was lifted.
* Open the gripper once the item stands on its own and touches nothing.
* Repeat for the middle item, then the right item.

The right arm is the idle arm for the whole of Step 1.

**Check:** the counter is empty, all three items stand upright on the staging strip, they are in the
same left to right order they started in, none of them touch, and none hang over the back table edge.
If an item leans, touches a neighbour, or hangs over, lift it clear with the left gripper and set it
down again once.

### Step 2: Take the cloth

**Goal:** the right gripper holds the cloth flat and ready.

* The **right gripper** comes down on the cloth on its park and closes on it so a broad flat side
  hangs below the gripper.
* Lift it clear of the park and bring it to the left end of the counter.

The left arm is the idle arm for the whole of Step 2.

**Check:** the cloth shows a flat face and does not hang by a corner or slip in the gripper. If it
does, set it back on the park and take it again. Two tries at most. If the cloth still will not hold
flat, end the episode and fix the cloth in the reset.

### Step 3: Wipe the counter

**Goal:** the whole counter is clean.

* With the **right gripper**, set the flat face of the cloth down at the left end of the counter, as
  far back on it as the staging strip allows.
* Draw it left to right along the full length of the counter in one pass. Keep it on the table the
  whole way. Press only hard enough to lift the dirt.
* Lift at the right end, go back to the left end, and run a second pass in front of the first. The
  two passes overlap, so the whole counter is covered.
* Keep the cloth on the counter. Do not run it onto the staging strip, do not push dirt over the
  front table edge, and do not lift it in the middle of a pass.

The left arm is the idle arm for the whole of Step 3.

**Check:** no dirt is left anywhere on the counter, including the strip along the back of it and both
ends. If a mark is left, run one short extra pass over it. Do not go on with dirt on the counter.

### Step 4: Park the cloth

**Goal:** the cloth is back on its park and the right gripper is empty.

* The **right gripper** carries the cloth to the cloth park, lays it down flat, opens, and lifts
  clear.
* Return the right arm to its home.

The left arm is the idle arm for the whole of Step 4.

**Check:** the cloth lies fully on its park, not draped over the table, over an item, or over the
staging strip, and the right gripper is empty before any item comes forward.

### Step 5: Bring the three items forward again

**Goal:** all three items stand on the clean counter, each one on its start spot.

Work left to right, one item per trip. Finish one item before starting the next.

* The **left gripper** comes down from above and closes on the appliance handle, or on the body of
  one of the other two items, and lifts it straight up clear of the staging strip.
* Carry it forward and set it down on the counter, on the spot it came from, with a narrow gap
  between it and the front table edge.
* Do not turn the item as you carry it, do not carry it over an item already placed, and do not push
  it forward until it reaches the front table edge.
* Open the gripper once the item stands on its own, clear of the front table edge and clear of the
  item already placed to its left.
* Repeat for the middle item, then the right item.

The right arm is the idle arm for the whole of Step 5.

**Check:** all three items stand on the clean counter in the order they started in, the staging strip
is empty, and nothing hangs over the front table edge or over the back of the counter. If an item
leans, touches a neighbour, or hangs over, lift it clear with the left gripper and set it down again
once.

### Step 6: Straighten the row

**Goal:** the three items form a neat row.

Work left to right, one item at a time, with the **left gripper** only.

* Close lightly on the item without lifting it, on the handle for the appliance and on the body for
  the other two. Set the gap in front of the first item by eye.
* For each later item, nudge it forward or back until its gap matches the items already set, then
  nudge it sideways until the gaps to its neighbours look the same.
* Open the gripper and move to the next item.
* Use two small nudges per item at most.
* Never lift an item to move it, and never turn it. The direction the appliance handle points is not
  judged.

The right arm is the idle arm for the whole of Step 6.

**Check:** the gap in front of every item looks the same along the row, no two items touch, nothing
hangs over the front table edge, and the left to right order is unchanged.

### Step 7: End the episode

* Confirm the counter: no dirt anywhere on it.
* Confirm the row: all three items stand on the counter in their start order, and the staging strip
  is empty.
* Confirm the gaps: the gap in front of every item looks the same, no two items touch, and nothing
  hangs over the front table edge or the back of the counter.
* Confirm the cloth lies flat on its park.
* Fix a failed check only with the fix written in its step. If no fix is written, or the fix would
  mean going back through the order, end the episode and reset instead.
* Return both arms home with grippers open, then stop recording.

## After the episode: reset the workspace

This reset is not recorded.

1. Lift the three items off the counter and put dirt back on it, including dirt where the items
   stand.
2. Stand the three items back on the counter, each on its own spot, in the same left to right order,
   pushed out of line with uneven gaps.
3. Check the appliance handle is still firm, and replace the appliance if it is loose. Replace any
   item that will not stand upright on its own.
4. Rinse or replace the cloth, fold it flat and damp, and lay it on the cloth park.
5. Wipe the staging strip and clear any bits off the table.
6. Run both Setup checklists again.

## SOP violations

These are actions that break the SOP and are reviewed side by side in the review tool.

### How to record a violation in review

For each violation, record the **start timestamp**, **violation name**, and **SOP rule broken**. The
visible cue is what the reviewer sees. The coaching note is for retraining and is not an annotation
label.

### Episode handling

Tag every violation with its timestamp and name. Keep the episode with the violation tag. Do not
delete it just because a rule was broken.

### Violations

**Violation: Wrong arm used for an action**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper touches the cloth, the right gripper touches an item, or a step
  that names one gripper is done with the other one.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, each gripper does only its own actions.
* **Coaching note:** left moves items, right handles the cloth. The roles never swap.

**Violation: Idle arm gets in the way**

* **Visible cue:** the arm not named in a step comes in, steadies an item, holds the cloth flat, or
  helps the working arm, or both arms work at the same time.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, one arm works while the other waits at home.
* **Coaching note:** one arm works, the other waits at home. Pull back before the other arm goes in.

**Violation: Item dragged instead of lifted clear**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper pushes, slides, or scrapes an item across the table between the
  counter and the staging strip instead of lifting it clear and carrying it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 and 5, lift the item straight up and carry it.
* **Coaching note:** the item leaves the table before it moves. Dragging smears the counter and tips
  the item.

**Violation: More than one item moved in one trip**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper lifts, carries, or pushes two or more items together.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 and 5, one item per trip.
* **Coaching note:** exactly one item per trip, every time.

**Violation: Wrong grip on an item**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper closes on the appliance body instead of its handle, or closes on
  the top or the rim of one of the other two items instead of on the body, or an item is turned in the
  air while it is carried.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1, 5, and 6, take the appliance by its handle and the other two items by
  the body, and do not turn an item.
* **Coaching note:** handle for the appliance, body for the other two. Come down from above and carry
  the item the way it was standing.

**Violation: Counter wiped with an item still on it**

* **Visible cue:** a pass starts while an item is still standing on the counter, or the right gripper
  wipes around an item instead of across the empty counter.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3, the counter is wiped only when it is empty.
* **Coaching note:** clear all three items first. Wiping around an item leaves a dirty ring.

**Violation: Counter only partly wiped**

* **Visible cue:** the counter gets one pass instead of two, the two passes leave a strip untouched,
  the strip along the back of the counter or an end of it is missed, the cloth lifts in the middle of
  a pass, or dirt is still visible and the items come forward anyway.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3, two overlapping passes cover the whole counter.
* **Coaching note:** two passes, the first along the back, cloth touching the table the whole way.

**Violation: Cloth bunched instead of held flat**

* **Visible cue:** the cloth is held by a corner, balled up, or dragged edge on, so only a narrow line
  of it touches the table.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 2 and 3, the cloth is held flat.
* **Coaching note:** gather the cloth so a broad flat face lies on the table. A bunched cloth cleans a
  line, not a counter.

**Violation: Cloth set down off its park**

* **Visible cue:** the right gripper lays the cloth on the counter, on the staging strip, or on an
  item instead of on its park.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 4, the cloth is only ever set down on its park.
* **Coaching note:** the cloth rests on its park and nowhere else.

**Violation: Item brought forward too early**

* **Visible cue:** an item goes back on the counter before the wipe is finished, before dirt is gone,
  or while the right gripper is still holding the cloth.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 5, the items come forward only after the counter is clean and the cloth is
  parked.
* **Coaching note:** clean counter, cloth parked, then the items. Never the other way round.

**Violation: Item put back in the wrong spot**

* **Visible cue:** an item ends up on a spot other than the one it started on, or two items are
  swapped from their start order.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 5 and 6, each item goes back to its own start spot and the left to right
  order never changes.
* **Coaching note:** each item goes straight back. This task never sorts the row.

**Violation: Row left uneven, touching, or hanging over**

* **Visible cue:** two items stand against each other, the gaps along the finished row look clearly
  different, an item sits much further back than its neighbours, or an item hangs over the front table
  edge or the back of the counter.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 6, the row ends even with nothing touching and nothing hanging over.
* **Coaching note:** set each gap as you go, then read the whole row left to right before you finish.

**Violation: Item dropped, knocked over, or hit**

* **Visible cue:** an item or the cloth falls, an item topples on the counter or the staging strip, an
  arm knocks a placed item on the way in or out, or an item is pushed off a table edge.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, move in and out clear and open the gripper only on a stable
  placement.
* **Coaching note:** check the path and the landing spot before you move, and come down onto the item
  from above.

**Violation: Wrong episode ending**

* **Visible cue:** the episode ends with a failed check not fixed, dirt on the counter, an item left
  on the staging strip, the cloth off its park, an arm near home but not at it, or a gripper not fully
  open.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 7, confirm the counter, the row, the gaps, and the cloth, then return both
  arms home with grippers open, then stop recording.
* **Coaching note:** confirm, home, stop.

### Failures that are not violations

These failures are not caused by how the task was run. Log them as system issues, discard the
episode, and never use them for coaching.

* **Recording stopped or paused during the episode** (recording system).
* **Camera dropped frames or lost its feed** (capture system).
* **Hardware fault on an arm:** gripper failure, drift, controller caused collision, or motor error.
* **Faulty item:** an item that will not stand upright on its own or slips out of a correct grip, or
  an appliance whose handle is loose or comes away. Replace it before the next episode.
* **Faulty cloth:** a cloth that is torn, shedding, dry at episode start, or so loaded with dirt that
  a correct wipe only smears it. Replace it before the next episode.
* **Damaged table:** a stain, scratch, or burn in the surface itself that does not lift under a
  correct wipe. Move the counter or replace the surface before the next episode.
* **An area shifts during the episode**, or the run turns out to sit outside an arm's comfortable
  reach, so part of the counter, the staging strip, or an item cannot be reached without stretching or
  folding the arm.

## Annotation subtasks (from SOP)

1. Move one item from the counter back to the staging strip
2. Take the cloth from the cloth park
3. Wipe the counter
4. Return the cloth to the cloth park
5. Bring one item forward from the staging strip onto the counter
6. Straighten one item in the row
7. Return both arms home and end the episode
