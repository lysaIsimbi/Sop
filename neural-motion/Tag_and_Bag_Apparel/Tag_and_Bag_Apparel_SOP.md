# Tag and Bag Apparel SOP

One episode tags, folds, bags, seals, and sorts two apparel items. Each garment comes off its stage
into the working area, takes one swift tag at its seam from the mock tag gun, is folded into a packet
on the table, is slid into a poly bag, is sealed at the flap, and goes onto the spot matching the size
on its label. One garment is staged on the left and one on the right, and the left one is worked first.

The order never changes within a garment: stage, tag, fold, bag, seal, tray. No garment is folded
before it is tagged, no garment goes into a bag before it is folded, no bag is carried to the tray
before its flap is sealed, and no bag goes down until the size on that garment's label has been
read.

The left gripper brings the left garment in, takes the poly bags, and holds whatever the right gripper
is working on. The right gripper brings the right garment in and does the tagging, the folding, the
insert, the seal, and the placing on the tray. Neither gripper takes the other's stage, nothing is folded or opened in the air, nothing is
turned in the air, and a gripper opens only over the spot the thing in it is going to.

## Setup

Complete both checklists before starting an episode.

### Hardware checklist

1. Cameras are on and recording.
2. The environment camera shows the whole table: the left garment stage at the left edge, the right
   garment stage at the right edge, the bag stack at the front left, the tag gun spot right of the
   working area, the working area at the center, and the tray at the back center.
3. The working area is visible from above, so every fold, every insert, and every seal can be seen.
4. Both arms are at home with grippers open.
5. The table is bare apart from the garments, the poly bags, the mock tag gun, and the tray.
6. The left arm reaches the left garment stage, the bag stack, and the working area. The right arm
   reaches the right garment stage, the tag gun spot, the working area, and the tray. Neither arm
   needs the other's zones.

### Materials checklist

1. Two **garments** of soft folded apparel, each lying flat and square: one at the left garment
   stage and one at the right garment stage.
2. Every garment lies hem edge toward the front edge, collar edge away, with its **size label** face
   up and readable from above.
3. The two sizes vary between episodes. Both garments carrying the same size in one episode is
   normal.
4. Two **poly bags** lie flat in one stack at the front left, both facing the same way, each with
   its **bag flap** lying back flat and its adhesive strip exposed, clean, and facing up, so the
   strip meets the bag when the flap is folded over.
5. One **mock tag gun** lies flat on the tag gun spot with its tip pointing toward the working area,
   loaded with two **swift tags**, handle clear so a gripper can take it from above.
6. One **tray** sits at the back center, empty and square to the table. Its surface carries three
   **size spots** in a row across it, left to right, with nothing marking them. The spots take S, M,
   and L in that order, and the order never changes between episodes. Each spot has room for two
   sealed bags lying flat, one on top of the other.
7. The working area at the center of the table is clear and dry.

### Workspace layout

* **Left garment stage:** left edge, one garment lying flat.
* **Right garment stage:** right edge, one garment lying flat.
* **Bag stack:** front left, two poly bags flat in one stack.
* **Tag gun spot:** the clear patch right of the working area, the mock tag gun lying flat with its
  tip toward the working area.
* **Working area:** the center of the table, where every tag press, fold, insert, and seal happens.
* **Tray:** back center, three unmarked size spots running left to right across it, taking S, M, and
  L in that order.

Everything the right gripper works on is staged on the right or in the center. The garments and the
bags the left gripper carries are staged on the left. No carried thing crosses the midline.

## Vocabulary

* **Arm assignment:** the left gripper takes the left stage garment and the poly bags, and holds
  whatever the right gripper is working on. The right gripper takes the right stage garment, works
  the tag gun, makes both folds, slides the packet into the bag, seals the flap, and places the
  sealed bag. Neither gripper takes the other's stage and neither takes over the other's work.
* **Garment:** one apparel item. Its **hem edge** is the edge nearest the front edge as it lies in
  the working area and its **collar edge** is the edge farthest from it.
* **Size label:** the printed size on the outside of a garment. It is read once, in the working area
  before the folds, and it decides the size spot.
* **Size spot:** the place on the tray a sealed bag belongs. Nothing marks the spots, so the spot
  comes from its position across the tray: the left third takes S, the middle third takes M, and the
  right third takes L. The order is the same in every episode and is never read off the tray.
* **Tag seam:** the side seam on the right edge of the garment as it lies in the working area, level
  with the middle of that edge. Every tag press is made there and nowhere else.
* **Mock tag gun:** the plastic tool that carries the swift tags. It is held by the handle, taken
  off a flat surface, carried level with the tip already pointing forward, and laid back down flat
  with the tip toward the working area. It is never turned in the air.
* **Tag press:** the tip set on the tag seam, pressed against it, held for 2 seconds, and lifted
  straight off. One press attaches one swift tag and the tag is treated as attached from that press.
* **Poly bag:** one flat bag holding one folded garment. Its **bag mouth** is the open end, its
  **top wall** is the upper face of the bag above the mouth, and its **bag flap** is the lip beyond
  the mouth, carrying an exposed adhesive strip on its upper face. The strip is exposed from
  staging, so no flap is ever peeled during an episode.
* **Sealed bag:** the flap folded back over the bag mouth onto the top wall and pressed along its
  length, so the adhesive strip has taken hold across the full width, no corner is lifting, and the
  flap stays down when the bag body is nudged.
* **Folded packet:** the garment after both folds, lying flat on the table with no layer standing up
  or hanging loose, and no wider than the bag mouth.
* **Flat hold:** the left gripper closed and resting on a garment, a packet, or a bag body to keep
  it from sliding while the right gripper works. During a fold it lifts clear as the incoming layer
  reaches it, so the fold lands flat.
* **Lift:** coming straight down onto the grasp point, closing, and raising straight up until there
  is daylight under the thing, before anything moves sideways.
* **Lower on:** carrying level over the size spot, lowering straight down until the bag is resting
  on the tray, and releasing once it is resting. Nothing is dropped from height.
* **Settled:** the thing stays put for 2 seconds after the gripper lifts clear, with nothing
  sliding, springing open, or hanging over an edge.
* **Release point:** over the working area, inside the bag mouth, on the tag gun spot, or over the
  size spot the bag is going onto. A gripper opens nowhere else.

## Steps

Steps 1 to 6 finish one garment. Run them twice, taking the left stage garment first, then the right
stage garment. Step 7 ends the episode once the second sealed bag is on the tray.

### Step 1: Stage one garment in the working area

**Goal:** one garment lying flat and square in the working area with its size label face up.

* For the first garment, the **left gripper** closes on the garment at the **left garment stage**,
  lifts it straight up, carries it level to the **working area**, lays it flat with the hem edge
  toward the front edge, and releases.
* For the second garment, the **right gripper** does the same from the **right garment stage**.
* Read the size label before moving on. It decides the size spot in Step 6.

Take one garment per trip and finish it before the next one comes in. Neither gripper reaches the other
stage.

**Check:** the garment lies flat and square in the working area, hem edge toward the front edge, no edge
folded under itself, and the size label face up and readable. If it lands bunched or folded over
itself, lift it clear and lay it down again.

### Step 2: Tag the seam

**Goal:** one swift tag pressed onto the tag seam and the gun back on its spot.

#### 2.1 Take the gun

* The **right gripper** closes on the handle of the **mock tag gun**, lifts it straight up off the
  tag gun spot, and carries it level to the working area with the tip already pointing forward.

#### 2.2 Press the seam

* The **left gripper** takes a **flat hold** on the garment at its left edge and keeps it for the
  whole press.
* The **right gripper** sets the tip on the **tag seam**, presses it against the seam, holds for 2
  seconds, and lifts straight off.
* Do not drag the tip along the seam, and press nowhere but the tag seam.

#### 2.3 Return the gun

* The **right gripper** carries the gun level back to the **tag gun spot**, lays it down flat with
  the tip toward the working area, and releases.
* The **left gripper** releases the flat hold once the gun is clear of the garment.

**Check:** one press was made on the tag seam, the garment is still flat and square with the size label
face up, and the gun lies flat on its spot with the tip toward the working area.

### Step 3: Fold the garment

**Goal:** a folded packet lying flat in the working area.

Both folds are made on the table. Never lift the garment to fold it in the air.

#### 3.1 Fold right edge to left edge

* The **left gripper** takes a **flat hold** at the garment's left edge.
* The **right gripper** pinches the middle of the right edge, drags it horizontally left across the
  garment, and lays it on the left edge, then releases.
* The **left gripper** lifts clear as the incoming edge reaches it.

**Expected state:** a narrow rectangle with the folded spine on the left.

#### 3.2 Fold collar edge to hem edge

* The **left gripper** takes a **flat hold** on the near half.
* The **right gripper** pinches the middle of the collar edge, drags it toward the front edge over
  the near half, and lays it on the hem edge, then releases.
* The **left gripper** lifts clear as the incoming edge reaches it.

**Check:** the packet lies flat with no layer standing up or hanging loose and is no wider than the bag
mouth. If a layer stands proud or the packet is too wide for the mouth, open the last fold, lay the
layer flat, and fold it again.

### Step 4: Bag the garment

**Goal:** the whole folded packet inside one poly bag with the flap still free.

#### 4.1 Lay a bag out

* The **left gripper** closes on the near edge of the top **poly bag** on the bag stack, lifts it
  straight up, carries it level to the working area, and lays it flat just beyond the folded packet,
  away from the front edge, with the **bag mouth** and **bag flap** toward the front edge, then
  releases.

Take one bag per trip. The flap stays lying back flat with its adhesive strip facing up until Step 5.
Nothing is set down on the strip.

#### 4.2 Slide the packet in

* The **left gripper** pinches the middle of the **top wall** at the bag mouth and lifts it just
  enough for the mouth to stand open.
* The **right gripper** closes on the near edge of the **folded packet**, lifts it, brings it level
  to the open mouth, and slides it in away from the front edge until the whole packet is inside and
  clear of the mouth, then releases inside the bag and withdraws.
* The **left gripper** lays the top wall back down flat and releases.

**Check:** the whole packet is inside the bag with no part of it standing proud of the mouth, the bag
lies flat, and the flap is still lying back flat toward the front edge with its adhesive strip facing
up and clear. If the packet is proud of the mouth, hold the mouth open again and slide it the rest of
the way in.

### Step 5: Seal the bag flap

**Goal:** a sealed bag lying flat in the working area.

* The **left gripper** takes a **flat hold** on the bag body beyond the mouth and keeps it for the
  whole seal.
* The **right gripper** closes on the near edge of the **bag flap**, folds it away from the front
  edge over the **bag mouth**, and lays it down on the **top wall** so the adhesive strip meets the
  bag.
* Press along the flap from one end to the other until the strip has taken hold across the full
  width and no corner is lifting.
* Release the right gripper, then the left.

The flap is folded once and laid down once. Do not lift a stuck flap to place it again, because the
strip loses its hold on a second try.

**Check:** the flap lies flat on the top wall over the bag mouth, the adhesive strip has taken hold
across the full width, no corner is lifting, and the flap stays down when the bag body is nudged. If a
corner lifts, press along it again.

### Step 6: Place the bagged garment on the tray by size

**Goal:** the sealed bag lying flat on the **size spot** for the size read from that garment, flap face
up.

* Take the **size spot** from the size read in Step 1: left third for S, middle third for M, right
  third for L. Nothing marks the spots, so the spot is counted off from the left end of the tray
  every time.
* The **right gripper** closes on the near edge of the **sealed bag**, lifts it straight up, carries
  it level to the **tray**, and lowers it onto that spot until it is resting on the tray or on the
  bag already there.
* Release once it is resting.

The bag goes down the way it was sealed, flap face up. Nothing is turned in the air, and no bag is
pressed down after it is resting.

**Check:** the bag lies flat on the size spot for its size, counting from the left end of the tray, flap
face up, nothing hangs over the edge of the tray, and the bag already on that spot has not been
disturbed.

### Step 7: Confirm the two bagged garments and end the episode

* Confirm the tray holds two sealed bags, each lying flat on the size spot for its size with the
  flap face up, and confirm both garment stages and the bag stack are empty, the mock tag gun lies
  flat on its spot with the tip toward the working area, and the working area is bare.
* Return both arms home, clear of the tray and the working area, then stop recording.

## After the episode: reset the workspace

This reset is not recorded.

1. Lift the two sealed bags off the tray and set the tray back at the back center, bare, square to
   the table, and in the same left to right orientation every time. Nothing marks the spots, so a
   tray turned end for end silently reverses S and L.
2. Peel each flap back off the bag, lay it flat again with its adhesive strip facing up, and slide
   the garment out.
3. Shake each garment out flat and check it for tears and for a readable size label. Replace any
   garment that is torn or whose label cannot be read.
4. Lay one garment flat at the left garment stage and one at the right garment stage, both hem edge
   toward the front edge with the size label face up.
5. Vary the sizes and which garment goes to which stage between episodes rather than running the
   same layout every time. The count stays at two garments and two bags.
6. Stack two poly bags flat at the front left, both facing the same way, each flap lying back flat
   with its adhesive strip facing up and clean. Test each strip with a fingertip and swap in a fresh
   bag if it has lost its tack, picked up lint, or torn at the fold.
7. Reload the mock tag gun with two swift tags and lay it flat on the tag gun spot with the tip
   toward the working area.
8. Wipe the working area and confirm the surface is clean and dry. A dusty table lets a garment
   slide under a flat hold.
9. Run both Setup checklists again.

## SOP violations

These are actions that break the SOP and are reviewed side by side in the review tool.

### How to record a violation in review

For each violation, record the **start timestamp**, **violation name**, and **SOP rule broken**. The
visible cue is what the reviewer sees. The coaching note is for retraining and is not an annotation
label.

### Episode handling

Tag every violation with its timestamp and name. Keep the episode with the violation tag. Do
not delete it just because a rule was broken.

### Violations

**Violation: Wrong order**

* **Visible cue:** a garment is folded before the tag press, a packet goes into a bag before both
  folds are made, a bag is carried to the tray before the flap is sealed, or the tag gun comes out
  again after the folds have started.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, the order within a garment never changes: stage, tag, fold,
  bag, seal, tray.
* **Coaching note:** stage, tag, fold, bag, seal, tray. Finish the step you are in before you start
  the next one.

**Violation: Garments taken out of order**

* **Visible cue:** the right stage garment is worked before the left stage garment is sealed and on
  the tray, or both garments are brought into the working area before either is finished.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 1, take the left stage garment first, then the right stage garment, and
  take one garment per trip.
* **Coaching note:** left stage empties first. One garment in the working area at a time.

**Violation: Arms swapped roles**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper takes the right stage garment, works the tag gun, makes a fold,
  slides the packet in, seals a flap, or reaches the tray; or the right gripper takes the left stage
  garment or a poly bag.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, the left gripper takes the left stage garment and the bags and
  holds what the right gripper works on, and the right gripper tags, folds, inserts, seals, and
  places.
* **Coaching note:** each arm stays on its own side. Nothing crosses the midline.

**Violation: More than one garment or bag handled at once**

* **Visible cue:** both garments are carried in together, two poly bags come off the stack together,
  or a garment is moved while the tag gun is still in the other gripper.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 and 4, one garment per trip and one bag per trip.
* **Coaching note:** one garment, one tag, one bag, one spot. Never bundle a cycle.

**Violation: Garment not laid flat and square**

* **Visible cue:** the garment is released in the working area bunched, folded over itself, turned
  across the table instead of hem edge toward the front edge, or with the size label face down, and
  the tag press is made on it anyway.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 1, the garment lies flat and square with the hem edge toward the front
  edge and the size label face up, and a bad landing is corrected by lifting it clear and laying it
  down again.
* **Coaching note:** square it up before you reach for the gun. Everything after this lands wrong
  otherwise.

**Violation: Tag pressed off the seam**

* **Visible cue:** the gun tip meets the middle of the garment, the collar edge, the hem edge, or
  the left edge instead of the tag seam on the right edge, or more than one press is made on one
  garment.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 2.2, one press on the tag seam and nowhere else.
* **Coaching note:** find the seam on the right edge first, then bring the tip down on it.

**Violation: Press not held, or the tip dragged**

* **Visible cue:** the tip touches the seam and comes away at once, the tip is drawn along the seam
  while pressed, or the gun is lifted away sideways instead of straight off.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 2.2, press the tip against the seam, hold for 2 seconds, and lift
  straight off without dragging.
* **Coaching note:** down, hold two, straight up. The tag does not seat on a tap.

**Violation: Garment not steadied during the tag press**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper is off the garment while the gun presses, or the garment slides,
  rucks, or lifts with the gun on the way out.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 2.2, the left gripper takes a flat hold at the left edge and keeps it
  for the whole press.
* **Coaching note:** the flat hold goes on before the tip touches and comes off after the gun is
  clear.

**Violation: Tag gun left off its spot or laid down turned**

* **Visible cue:** the gun is parked on the working area, on a garment, or on a stage between
  garments, is still in the gripper while a fold or an insert is made, or is laid back on its spot
  with the tip pointing away from the working area.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 2.3, carry the gun back to the tag gun spot and lay it down flat with
  the tip toward the working area before anything else is done.
* **Coaching note:** the gun goes home after every press, tip forward, so the next pick is clean.

**Violation: Garment lifted off the table to fold it**

* **Visible cue:** the garment leaves the table during a fold, an edge is carried over in the air,
  or the packet is shaped while hanging from a gripper.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3, both folds are made on the table and the garment is never lifted to
  fold it.
* **Coaching note:** drag the edge across the table. Nothing folds in the air.

**Violation: Folds made in the wrong order or direction**

* **Visible cue:** the collar to hem fold is made before the right to left fold, an edge is dragged
  the opposite way, or only one fold is made before the bag comes out.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3, fold the right edge left onto the left edge first, then the collar
  edge toward the front edge onto the hem edge.
* **Coaching note:** right to left, then far to near. Two folds, always in that order.

**Violation: Flat hold missed or left under the fold**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper never lands on the garment during a fold and the bottom layer
  drags along with the incoming edge, or the gripper is still on the garment when the incoming layer
  lands on top of it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 3, the left gripper takes a flat hold and lifts clear as the incoming
  layer reaches it.
* **Coaching note:** hold it down through the drag, then get out of the way just before it lands.

**Violation: Bag laid out the wrong way round**

* **Visible cue:** the bag is laid with the mouth and flap pointing away from the front edge or off
  to a side, or it is laid between the front edge and the packet instead of just beyond it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 4.1, lay the bag flat just beyond the folded packet, away from the front
  edge, with the bag mouth and flap toward the front edge.
* **Coaching note:** mouth toward you, bag beyond the packet. The packet only ever travels away from
  you.

**Violation: Bag mouth not held open during the insert**

* **Visible cue:** the packet is pushed at a closed bag, the left gripper is off the top wall while
  the packet goes in, or the mouth collapses and the packet rides over the bag instead of into it.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 4.2, the left gripper lifts the top wall at the mouth so the mouth
  stands open, and holds it while the packet slides in.
* **Coaching note:** open it first, then load it. A flat bag will not take a packet.

**Violation: Packet dropped in or left proud of the mouth**

* **Visible cue:** the gripper opens above the bag and the packet falls in, or the gripper releases
  with part of the packet still standing out of the mouth and the flap is sealed over it anyway.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 4.2, slide the packet in away from the front edge until the whole packet
  is inside and clear of the mouth, then release inside the bag.
* **Coaching note:** slide it all the way in and check the mouth before you let go.

**Violation: Flap not sealed**

* **Visible cue:** the flap is left lying back, folded over but never pressed, pressed only at one
  end, or lifting at a corner when the bag is carried, and the bag goes to the tray anyway.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 5, fold the flap over the bag mouth onto the top wall and press along it
  from one end to the other until the adhesive strip has taken hold across the full width with no
  corner lifting.
* **Coaching note:** press the whole length, then nudge the bag to see if it holds.

**Violation: Stuck flap lifted and laid down again**

* **Visible cue:** the flap is peeled back off the top wall after it has taken hold and folded down
  a second time, or it is worked loose and repositioned before the press.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 5, the flap is folded once and laid down once, and a stuck flap is not
  lifted to place it again.
* **Coaching note:** line the fold up before it lands. The strip will not hold a second time.

**Violation: Bag not held during the seal**

* **Visible cue:** the left gripper is off the bag body while the flap is folded or pressed, or the
  bag slides, rucks, or lifts under the press.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 5, the left gripper takes a flat hold on the bag body and keeps it for
  the whole seal.
* **Coaching note:** anchor the body first, release it last.

**Violation: Bag put on the wrong spot**

* **Visible cue:** a sealed bag goes onto a spot whose position does not match the size on that
  garment, counting S, M, and L from the left end of the tray, or the bag goes down without the size
  having been read while the garment lay flat.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 and 6, read the size label in the working area and lower the bag onto
  the size spot for that size, left for S, middle for M, right for L.
* **Coaching note:** read the size while the garment is still flat, and say it to yourself before
  you reach for the tray.

**Violation: Bag dropped onto the tray, pressed down, or laid flap face down**

* **Visible cue:** the gripper opens above the tray and the bag falls, the bag is pressed down after
  it is resting, it lands flap face down, or it hangs over the edge of the tray.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 6, lower the bag until it is resting, release once it is resting, flap
  face up, and never press a bag down after it is resting.
* **Coaching note:** take it all the way down, then let go. Leave it how it lands.

**Violation: Turned in the air**

* **Visible cue:** a garment, a packet, a bag, or the tag gun is rotated or flipped between the lift
  and the set down, so it lands facing a different way than it was carried.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, nothing is turned in the air; the orientation set on the table
  is the orientation it lands in.
* **Coaching note:** set it down and square it on the table. The wrist does not have the range to
  turn it in transit.

**Violation: Dragged instead of lifted**

* **Visible cue:** a garment, a bag, or the tag gun slides along the table still touching it, with
  no daylight under it in transit. The fold drags in Step 3 are not this.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1, 2, 4, and 6, lift straight up until there is daylight before moving
  sideways.
* **Coaching note:** the lift is its own motion, not the start of the carry.

**Violation: Regripped in the air**

* **Visible cue:** the gripper shifts, rolls, or seats its hold again on a garment, a packet, a bag,
  or the tag gun without setting it down first.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, set it down before taking a new hold.
* **Coaching note:** put it down before you take a new hold.

**Violation: Gripper released over the wrong place**

* **Visible cue:** a gripper opens over a garment stage, over the bag stack, over the tray while
  carrying a garment or a loose bag, or over open table.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, a gripper opens over the working area, inside the bag mouth, on
  the tag gun spot, or over the size spot the bag is going onto, and nowhere else.
* **Coaching note:** know where it lands before you open the gripper.

**Violation: Placement corrected by shoving**

* **Visible cue:** a gripper nudges, pats, or slides a garment, a packet, a bag, or the tag gun into
  position after releasing it, instead of lifting it clear and placing it again.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, correct a bad placement by lifting the thing clear and placing
  it again.
* **Coaching note:** lift it and lay it again, or leave it alone.

**Violation: Dropped, collided, or knocked something over**

* **Visible cue:** a garment, a bag, or the tag gun falls to the table or the floor short of its
  spot, the tray is struck and shifts, a sealed bag already on the tray is knocked off, the bag
  stack is scattered, or the two arms strike each other.
* **SOP rule broken:** Steps 1 to 6, keep each thing on a clear travel path and release only after a
  settled placement.
* **Coaching note:** check the path and the landing spot before you move.

**Violation: Wrong episode ending**

* **Visible cue:** the episode ends with a garment still on a stage, a bag unsealed or still in the
  working area, a loose bag on the stack, the tag gun off its spot, an arm away from home, an arm
  still over the tray or the working area.
* **SOP rule broken:** Step 7, confirm the two sealed bags on their matching spots and the empty
  stages, stack, and working area; return both arms home clear of the scene; then stop recording.
* **Coaching note:** confirm first. Homing is the last thing the arms do.

### Failures that are not violations

These failures are not caused by how the task was run. Log them as system issues, discard the episode, and never
use them for coaching.

* **Recording stopped or paused during the episode** (recording system).
* **Camera dropped frames or lost its feed** (capture system).
* **Hardware fault on an arm:** gripper failure, drift, controller caused collision, or motor error.
* **Defective garment:** torn mid episode, or a size label that cannot be read once it is flat in
  the working area. Replace it before the next episode.
* **Tag gun fault:** the gun runs out of swift tags before the second garment, the tip breaks, or
  the handle is too slick for the gripper to lift off a flat surface. Reload or replace it before
  the next episode.
* **Defective poly bag:** a torn bag, a mouth that will not stand open, a flap torn at the fold, or
  an adhesive strip that will not take hold under a correct press because it has gone dead or picked
  up lint. Swap in a fresh bag before the next episode.
* **Tray shifts out of its zone** during the episode, is turned end for end so the spot order runs
  the wrong way, or is too small to take both sealed bags without one overhanging an edge. Square
  the tray up before the next episode.
* **Garment or bag slides** under a correct flat hold because the table is dusty or slick. Wipe the
